# Privacy policy for app 1.11.0: daily activity count, Cycle supplies + import, web place search

## Why
Three things changed how data is handled since 1.10.0, and the privacy
policy does not say them yet. It must be true and **deployed before build
17 goes into review**:
1. **Daily activity count** (api `58b9534`, 2026-10-14): a middleware
   writes one `user_active_days` row per person, per (Vienna) day, per
   source (`ios`/`android`/`app`/`web`/`board`/`trmnl`/`widget`/`watch`/
   `background`/`other`) with the app version and the first time seen. The
   owner dashboard (dashi) and a morning email show totals. Rows are pruned
   after 400 days (`active-days:prune`), kept with `user_id = null` when an
   account is purged (`AccountPurger`), and listed in the data export
   (`ExportUserDataJob`). The app sends `X-Daili-Client` + `X-Daili-App-Version`
   (app `c95b2b43`).
2. **Cycle** (app 2026-10-15/023, 026, 027): CSV **import** of an own export;
   "Show on my calendar" (device-only); the **supplies suggestion** — only
   the item names she ticks are sent, as ordinary items, to one of HER
   private shopping lists (or a new private list "Personal").
3. **Web place search** (api `8c943f5`, daili-web weather): the web app's
   city search and "use my location" go through our server to the **Apple
   Maps Server API** (search text or a position rounded to 2 decimals +
   language; results cached 24 h by query/rounded point; query and
   coordinates never in the Laravel log).

The English and German texts below were written by Planning Claude against
the code. **Use them word for word.** If a sentence is not true for the
code, stop and report it — do not quietly change it. Exception: the list of
sources in the activity paragraph may be corrected to the real ones.
