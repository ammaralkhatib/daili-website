Your family, on the same page.

One calm place for your family's week: a shared calendar, shopping lists and to-dos everyone can see. On every phone — and on the computer, too.

1 / 7 Calendar

One calendar for everyone

See the whole family's week and month at a glance — and never miss pickup again.

  • Color-coded events with categories, locations and notes
  • Repeating events done right — soccer every Tuesday, piano every other week
  • Reminders on every phone in the family
  • Public holidays for 35 countries, private events stay private
2 / 7 Shopping

Shopping lists that update live

Add "milk" on your phone — it appears on your partner's in the store, in about a second.

  • Shared lists the whole family can edit
  • Quantities, categories and notes per item
  • Checked items drop neatly to the bottom
3 / 7 To-dos

Every task has a name on it

No more "I thought you were doing it." Kids see their own tasks and can check them off.

  • Assignees, due dates and reminders
  • Works for chores, errands and everything between
4 / 7 Meals

Dinner, decided by Sunday

Plan breakfast to dinner for the whole week, straight from your family's own recipe box — then send the ingredients to a shopping list.

  • Weekly meal plan with four slots per day
  • Recipe box with photos and URL import
  • Add a recipe's ingredients to any list in one tap
5 / 7 Celebrations

Never miss a birthday again

Birthdays, anniversaries and family celebrations — remembered every year, with a countdown and reminders for everyone.

6 / 7 Private by design

Photos & documents stay on your phone

The Daili vault keeps family photos and important documents organized — stored only on your device, never uploaded to our servers.

7 / 7 Family

Parents, kids & grandma — all in

Invite your family with a simple 6-digit code or link. Parents are admins, kids and teens get their own login, and little ones without an email get a managed profile you can upgrade later.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What does Daili cost?

Nothing. Daili is free — no ads, no data selling. If we ever add paid extras, everything that is free today stays free.

Is there a web version?

Yes. Open app.daili.app in any browser on your computer and sign in with the same account.

Which languages does it speak?

The app speaks 19 languages, fully — including the reminders it sends.

Can my kids use it?

Yes — that's the point. Kids and teens get their own login and see their own tasks and events.
