FAQ

Questions, answered

What does Daili cost?

Nothing. Daili is free — no ads, no data selling. If we ever add paid extras, everything that is free today stays free.

Is there a web version?

Yes. Open app.daili.app in any browser on your computer and sign in with the same account. Calendar, lists, meals, birthdays and family are all there; photos and documents stay on your phone. The web app is in English and German.

Which languages does it speak?

The app speaks 19 languages, fully — including the reminders it sends: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish, Czech, Slovak, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Turkish, Romanian, Bulgarian, Greek and Ukrainian.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone and Android, from one shared family account. Everything syncs between them — and with the web app.

Can my kids use it?

Yes — that's the point. Kids and teens get their own login and see their own tasks and events. Little ones without an email address get a managed profile a parent controls, which can be upgraded to a real login later. Parents are admins and can remove a profile and its data at any time.

Does it work offline?

You can read your calendar, lists and to-dos without a connection, and changes sync as soon as you're back online. Photos and documents in the vault are always offline — they live only on your phone.

Can I bring my existing calendar?

Daili can show your phone's own calendar next to the family calendar, on your device. Connecting a Google or Outlook calendar is coming soon.

Where is my data stored?

Your calendar, lists and family data live on our server in the EU. Photos and documents in the vault never leave your phone. Details in the privacy policy.
