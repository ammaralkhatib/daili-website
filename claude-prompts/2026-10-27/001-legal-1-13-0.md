# Privacy policy for app 1.13.0: Copy to Daili, photo location data, language, old-address mail

## Why
App 1.13.0 (19) changes four things the privacy policy must say. It must be
true and **deployed before build 19 goes into review**:
1. **Copy to Daili** (app `d14a3ead`): on an event from the phone's calendar
   or a linked calendar (Google, subscribed ICS link) the user can tap
   **Copy to Daili**. The app opens the normal New event form, pre-filled with
   title, notes, place, all-day and start/end. Nothing is sent until the user
   taps Save; then it is a normal Daili event (stored on our server, family
   sees it unless private, connected AI apps can read it like any Daili
   event). Today the policy says phone-calendar events are "never uploaded"
   and that AI apps "never receive data from your connected Google calendars"
   — both need the honest exception. Ammar decided 2026-10-27: keep the
   feature for every calendar and disclose it.
2. **Photo location data** (app `8f49c1af`): the app removes EXIF data (incl.
   the GPS position) from every photo it uploads, on the phone, before upload.
3. **Language** (api `d37b180`, app `fb0641ad`): until the user picks a
   language in Settings, the app sends the phone's language to our server so
   e-mails and notifications use it (`locale_source` = auto / explicit).
4. **E-mail change** (api `d412758`): we send a notice to the OLD address when
   the sign-in e-mail changes.

The English and German texts below were written by Planning Claude against
the commits and reports. **Use them word for word.** If a sentence is not
true for the code (read the app files in `../familyplanner-app` and the api in
`~/Herd/familyplanner-api`), stop and report it — do not quietly change it.

## 1. English (`legal/privacy.en.html`)

a) Section 4, "Connected calendars (optional)" paragraph: in the sentence
"If you add your phone's calendar, those events are read and shown on your
device only — they are never uploaded to our servers, and your family cannot
see them." replace the full stop at the end with:
` — unless you copy one of them into Daili yourself (see "Copy to Daili" below).`

b) Section 4, insert right **after** the "Connected calendars" paragraph:

```html
<p id="copy-to-daili"><strong>Copy to Daili (optional).</strong> Events from your phone's calendar or from a linked calendar can't be changed in Daili. If you tap <em>Copy to Daili</em> on such an event, the app opens a new Daili event filled in with its title, place, times and notes (if it has any). Nothing is sent until you tap Save, and you can change or remove anything first. Once saved, the copy is a normal Daili event: it is stored on our server like any event you create, your family can see it unless you make it private, and AI apps you connect to Daili can read it like your other events. The original event stays where it is and is not changed. Legal basis: performance of the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).</p>
```

c) Section 4, insert right **before** the paragraph that starts
"Pictures, drawings and recordings in notes":

```html
<p id="photo-location"><strong>Location data in photos.</strong> Photos often carry hidden information, such as the place where they were taken. Before a photo you add leaves your phone — for example a profile picture, a recipe photo or a picture in a note — the app removes this hidden information. Only the picture itself is sent.</p>
```

d) Section 4, "Account data" paragraph: replace

e) Section 4, "E-mails" paragraph: replace

with

g) Section 5 (Google user data), "How we use it": after the sentence that
ends "…if your family connects one." add:
` If you tap <em>Copy to Daili</em> on one event, we also use that event to fill in a new Daili event for you (see "Copy to Daili" in section 4).`

h) Section 5, the line "AI apps you connect to Daili (see "AI apps you
connect" in section 4) never receive data from your connected Google

` The only exception is an event you copy into Daili yourself with <em>Copy to Daili</em>: once you save it, it is your own Daili event.`
