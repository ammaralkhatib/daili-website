# Privacy policy for app 1.12.0: AI avatar, Budget guests + read-only share link

## Why
Two features since 1.11.0 handle data in a way the privacy policy does not
say yet. It must be true and **deployed before build 18 goes into review**:
1. **AI avatar** (api `27e3224`, app `46bf72e2` `02d8f975` `e9bba36d`): the app
   sends a selfie (adults only, own avatar only, after a one-time "18 or
   older" confirmation stored as `users.adult_confirmed_at`) OR picked traits
   (age group, skin tone, hair, hair colour, beard, glasses, head covering, top
   colour; also for children — a child's photo is never sent) to Google Gemini,
   which draws 3 versions. The server never writes the selfie to disk or a log
   (privacy test in api report 2026-10-21/001). The chosen PNG is uploaded as a
   normal avatar. All versions are kept on the phone (`vault/u<id>/avatar_ai/`,
   newest 30 per person, in the phone's OS backup, erased with the account).
   `ai_usages` (api `2f8b8d4`) stores only ok/empty/failed/blocked per request,
   no content.
2. **Budget guests + share link** (api `c757a06` `be3f740`): a guest = a name
   + colour on ONE budget, no account, no e-mail, no push; in the GDPR export;
   deleted with the budget. The opt-in read-only page `api.daili.app/b/{token}`
   (no login, no cookies, `noindex`, `no-store`, `no-referrer`, throttled) shows
   the budget name, dates of a one-time budget, the split people with paid /
   share / balance, Who pays whom, and the shared expenses + settle-ups that
   count. Never: income, personal expenses, notes, payment methods, bills,
   limits, e-mails, avatars, other budgets, the family name. "Stop sharing"
   makes the old link 404 at once. The token is in nginx's access log (accepted).

## 1. English (`legal/privacy.en.html`)

a) Section 4, insert right **after** the "AI features (optional)" paragraph:

```html
<p id="avatar-ai"><strong>AI avatar (optional, Daili Plus).</strong> If you create an avatar with AI, the app sends either a selfie you take or choose (adults only, and only for their own avatar) or the looks you pick (for example age group, hair, skin tone, glasses or head covering) to Google's Gemini AI (Google Ireland Ltd.), which draws three versions. For children we never use a photo — only the looks you pick. The selfie is used only to draw these versions: our server does not save it and does not write it to any log, and Google does not use it to train its models. The version you choose is saved as your profile picture, like any picture you upload. All versions also stay on your phone (the newest 30 per person) and are part of your phone's own backup; they are deleted with your account. When you confirm that you are 18 or older, we store the date of that confirmation with your account. Legal basis: performance of the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).</p>
```

b) Section 4, insert right **after** the `id="budget"` paragraph:

```html
<p id="budget-guests"><strong>Budget guests and the guest link (optional).</strong> You can add guests to a budget — people without a Daili account, for example friends on a holiday. For a guest we store only the name you give them and a colour; guests get no account, no e-mails and no notifications. If you create a link for guests, anyone who has the link can open a read-only page without signing in. It shows the budget's name, the people who share the costs, who pays whom, and the shared expenses and settle-ups — never income, personal expenses, notes, payment methods, e-mail addresses or your family's name. The page sets no cookies and is hidden from search engines. You can stop sharing at any time; the old link then stops working at once. Like every request, the page's address (which contains the link) appears in our server's short-lived technical logs. Guests are part of the data export of the budget's creator and are deleted with the budget. Legal basis: performance of the contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).</p>
```
