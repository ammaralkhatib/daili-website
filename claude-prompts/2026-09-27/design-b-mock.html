01 — CALENDAR

One calendar for everyone.

See the whole family's week and month at a glance — and never miss pickup again. Color-coded events, repeats done right, reminders on every phone.

02 — SHOPPING

Lists that update live.

Add "milk" on your phone — it appears on your partner's in the store, in about a second. Quantities, categories and notes per item; checked items drop to the bottom.

03 — TO-DOS

Every task has a name on it.

No more "I thought you were doing it." Assignees, due dates and reminders — and kids see their own tasks and can check them off.

04 — MEALS

Dinner, decided by Sunday.

Plan breakfast to dinner from your family's own recipe box — import from a link or a YouTube video — then send the ingredients to a shopping list in one tap.

05 — CELEBRATIONS

Never miss a birthday again.

Birthdays, anniversaries and family celebrations — remembered every year, with a countdown and one reminder for everyone.

Plan it on Sunday evening. Live it all week. Five minutes at the kitchen table — and four phones know the plan.
