**6. Widgets per family — no text (the policy has no widgets sentence).** The prompt says to update it only if one exists. What the code really does:
- Storage is the iOS App Group `group.app.daili` / Android `HomeWidgetPreferences`, with keys `<key>.f<familyId>` (`widget_family_data.dart:14-31`). It is kept only for families a widget drew in the last 14 days.
- It is cleared at sign-out (`widget_data_writer.dart:347-357`).
- After you **leave** a family, the data goes only at the next app start, resume or family switch, not at once (`widget_family_data.dart:488-526`).
- The widgets themselves make no network calls. But the app **fetches** the other family's data from api.daili.app in the background to fill them (`headless_widget_refresh.dart:231,482`). So "never sent anywhere" is true, while "never fetched" would not be.

## Facts I could not confirm / left out
- Fact 1: "the activity list shows it came from the screen" is **not true for rewards** (no activity row), so the text doesn't claim it.
- Fact 2: tab counts are **not** gated in the browser, only on the server, and by the consent of the screen's creator (not the family's). The text says exactly that.
- Fact 3: the push goes to the other members of the **family**, not just the list's members. The switch is **on by default**. Nobody is stored as having marked an item urgent.
- Fact 5: there is no "like", only "Love it" / "Not for me". Notes have no author. Plan counts are not stored.
- Fact 6: not written (see above). Deletion after leaving a family is not instant.
- Fact 7: there is no place in the widget rows. The "colour" is an on/off, not a picked colour.
- Fact 9: "no new data" is not true (see above).
