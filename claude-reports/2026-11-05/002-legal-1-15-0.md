## Sentences that are not true (fix the text, then re-run)
Most important first. "Must fix" means the sentence is wrong. "Should fix" means it is incomplete or a little off.

**Must fix**

1. **Tips, hold-out (1d/2d):** "some accounts are chosen at random not to receive any for a few weeks". The accounts are not random, and there is no end date. The rule is: every user whose ID is a multiple of 10 never gets a tip, for as long as `NUDGES_HOLDOUT_ENABLED` is on (`config/nudges.php:16-20`, `NudgeRules.php:49-51`). There is no 8-week limit in the code. Suggested text: "about one account in ten, picked by a fixed rule, gets no tips while we measure whether they help."
2. **Tips, "only a few in total":** there is no lifetime limit. The 5 welcome tips go out at most once each, in days 1–20. The 3 win-back tips (after 14, 30 and 60 quiet days) can come again once the same tip is 180 days old. There is also a cap of 2 a week that the text doesn't mention, and tips only go to people who have not used Daili today or yesterday (`config/nudges.php:31-36`, `NudgeRules.php:66,102-118`). Report 001 found the same thing.
3. **Surveys, "you can skip it. If you answer, we store …" + "consent, given by answering":** closing the card with X also sends `answer: "dismissed"`. The server stores a full row for it (`home_survey_card.dart:97-111`, `SurveyAnswerController.php:26-38`). Every row, answered or not, also stores the **language** and the **platform** (iOS/Android/web). It also keeps the **user ID** while the account exists. A "dismissed" row can't be consent given by answering, so the legal basis needs a second look (for example: legitimate interest for the dismissed row). Also, on account deletion only `user_id` and `note` are cleared. `family_id` stays, so "only an anonymous count" is not fully true while the family still exists.
4. **Edit in Daili, phone copies "not updated automatically":** a new event saved without a link (for example from a phone-calendar copy, or an old app) is linked on the server anyway if its title and time exactly match one synced row the person can see. After that it follows that original (`EventController.php:369-374`, `CopyLinker.php`). So a phone event that is also in a linked Google calendar can end up being updated automatically.
5. **Edit in Daili, "Events you had copied before November 2026 were linked … once":** `calendar:link-copies` only looked at copies made **since 28 October 2026** (`CopyLinker::COPIES_SINCE`). The same matching also runs on every new event, not just once (see 4).

**Should fix**

6. **Edit in Daili, "a Google calendar or a calendar link":** Outlook events are linked too (`MicrosoftCalendarSource.php:226`). Also, if a whole linked calendar is deleted, the link is cleared without telling anyone (`nullOnDelete`). That doesn't fit "the app tells you and lets you keep or delete".
7. **Tips, the facts list:** the selector also uses whether you have a phone that received a push in the last 90 days, the app version, whether you are admin or owner, the family size, your daily-summary setting and your own tip history (`NudgeFactsLoader.php:69-89`). "Uses only facts it already holds: …" reads as a complete list.
8. **Tips, web path:** in the web app the page is called **"Profile & settings"**, not Settings (`daili-web` `en.ts:230`).
9. **Help improve, "which screens … and a few moments":** the counter also counts every API error shown to you (`error.<code>`), the Plus sheet being shown, and the invite copy/share buttons (`error_message.dart:41`, `plus_sheet.dart:172`). It is still numbers only. Also, finished days are sent at app start or resume, at most once an **hour**, not "once a day" (`usage_sender.dart:58,77-86`). After account deletion the rows stay one by one (day, key, platform, version) with no user ID; they are not merged into totals.
10. **Help improve, the Settings row:** the row shows only for an admin, or once the switch is on (`usage_sharing_settings_row.dart:14-18`). The Home sheet goes to any adult who hasn't answered, not only "existing parents". The text still works as written ("switch it off in the same place" is true once it is on).
11. **Crashlytics:** no user ID, custom keys or logs: true, and a test guards it. But the report also includes the error **message** (`recordError(error, stack)`), which could contain some data. It probably also carries Crashlytics' own installation ID. You could add "the error message" to the list.

**Checked and true:** AI for adults (server rule, the one-time question, the stored date), share to event (Gemini once, nothing stored, 1 action only when events are found), "Only me" calendar links, Refresh now (no new data), nothing counted in Cycle, OFF deletes counts + unsent crash reports, the web counts nothing, export fields, the original event is never changed.
