## Device frames: D2, not D1
The `bezels/` folder holds **DMGs** (iPhone 18, Watch Series 11, iPad Pro), not PNGs. Mounting a DMG shows Apple's licence agreement, and it has to be accepted first. I did not accept a licence on your behalf. Also, the licence text says the resources may not be used to create or distribute "website content". So I built **D2**: CSS frames. The phone's outer radius is 16 % of its width. The frame is 11 px at 300 px and scales with the phone (container units). It has a 1 px darker outer line and a black bezel inside the metal. The screen radius is outer − frame. The watch case is 0.83 : 1 (measured: 0.830) and has a thicker band. There are no hole rects to report, since no bezel was used. **Your step if you want D1:** accept the licence, export the PNGs, check whether that licence permits use on the website, then run D1.
