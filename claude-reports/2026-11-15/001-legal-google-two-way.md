> Only if you tap "Connect Google Calendar" and agree on Google's consent screen, Daili gets read-only access with these permissions:
> - `calendar.calendarlist.readonly` — the list of your calendars (name and colour), so you can choose which calendars Daili shows;
> - `calendar.events.readonly` — the events of the calendars you chose;
> - `openid` and `email` — the e-mail address of the Google account, so you can see which account is connected.
>
> **Two-way sync (optional).** Two-way sync is being rolled out step by step, so it may not be offered to you yet. Only if you switch one of your calendars to "Two-way" (in the app: Choose calendars → Sync → Two-way; the app first explains what this means, then Google's own page asks you) does Daili ask for one more permission:
> - `calendar.events` — view and edit events on all your calendars.
>
> Google gives this permission for all calendars of your Google account, but Daili writes only to the calendars you switched to Two-way, and only to calendars Google lets you edit. If you do not allow it on Google's page, your connection stays read-only.
>
> Without Two-way sync, Daili only reads your Google Calendar. With Two-way sync, Daili creates, changes or deletes an event in Google only when you yourself add, change or delete that event in Daili. Daili never changes your Google Calendar by itself. Only the person who linked the calendar can do this: other family members, a family wall screen, a TRMNL display and connected AI apps can never change anything in Google. For this, Daili sends Google only the event's title, place, notes, start and end, whether it is all-day and its time zone — for a new repeating event also its repeat rule, and for a new event a technical event ID that Daili makes up. Nothing else goes to Google: people, reminders and categories stay in Daili. Events that have guests, invitations from other people, events Google locks and special Google events (for example birthdays or out of office) stay view-only in Daili, and Daili never sends invitation or update e-mails to anyone. Switching a calendar back to "View only", removing it from Daili, disconnecting Google or deleting your Daili account deletes nothing in your Google Calendar.
>
> If you sign in with Google (section 4), we receive only your name, e-mail address and a technical user ID, and use them only to sign you in.
