## Fact check against the code

Every sentence matches the code:
- The key lives in the browser's localStorage (`daili.token`).
- Remove deletes the screen's Sanctum token at once (`BoardScreenRevoker`).
- Hidden areas: private events, lists, items and habits, Notes, Budget,
  Attachments, Activity and connected accounts (`config/board.php` allow-list
  + ScreenMode).
- Ticks name the person and are recorded as `meta.via_screen`.
- Device language: `devices.locale` from the app and the web. E-mails keep
  `users.locale`.

Two small notes. Neither one makes a sentence false:
- **"Unused set-up codes are deleted within a day":** `board-pairings:prune`
  runs every hour and deletes pairings older than 24 h, used or not. A code
  can therefore live up to about 25 h. A code's 10-minute window ends long
  before that, and its waiting key is wiped at once. I left the wording as
  written. Change it to "after about a day" if you want it exact.
- The "Browser notifications" paragraph sits in **section 3** (the web app),
  not section 4 as the prompt said. I edited it where it is.
