Cycle is off until you turn it on, and it's free. Each person decides for themselves. Nobody in your family sees your Cycle, or that you use it.

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Cycle**. Or pick the Cycle card when you first set up daili.
2. Tap **Start** and follow the steps.
3. While your period lasts, tap **Log today**, and **It ended** when it's over.

![Cycle with the ring on day 18, "Next period in 8–14 days", and the month. Today is circled.](cycle-start)

When a new period starts, or to log another day, tap that day in the month. Days to come can't be logged.

The next period is shown as a range of days, never one exact day. Predictions get better after 3 cycles. Past cycles are under **History**.

> Note: Estimates only. Cycle is not a medical device and not for birth control. More in the terms of use on daili.app.
