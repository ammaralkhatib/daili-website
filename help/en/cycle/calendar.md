The month under the card shows your period as a band: solid for days you logged, soft for probable days, striped for predicted days. Today has a dark ring.

![The Cycle month on period day 2: a band on Oct 15–16, soft days after it, and today ringed. Edit period days is circled.](cycle-calendar)

1. Tap a day. Switch on **Period today** and, if you like, pick **How much?**. **Spotting** is separate.
2. Add symptoms, like **Nausea** or **Low energy**, and a mood. Tap **Done**.
3. Use ‹ › to go to the day before or after. **Clear day** empties a day, with **Undo**.
4. To fix many days at once, tap **Edit period days** at the bottom of the calendar. Tap the days, then **Done**.

![The day sheet for Friday, October 16: Period today is on and Heavy is picked. The switch is circled.](cycle-day)

**History** on the Cycle screen shows a bar for each cycle, your shortest and longest cycle, and **Patterns** counted from what you logged.

> Note: Days to come can't be logged.
