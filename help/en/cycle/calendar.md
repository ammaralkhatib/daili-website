The month under the card shows your period as a band: solid for days you logged, soft for probable days, striped for predicted days. Today has a dark ring. ⓘ at the bottom explains the other marks.

![The Cycle month on period day 2: a band on Nov 12–13, soft days after it, and today ringed. The pencil at the top of the month is circled.](cycle-calendar)

1. Tap a day. Switch on **Period today** and, if you like, pick **Light**, **Medium** or **Heavy**. **Spotting** shows while **Period today** is off.
2. Tap a face for your mood, and add symptoms like **Nausea** or **Low energy**. Tap **Done**.
3. Use ‹ › to go to the day before or after. The bin next to **Done** clears a day, with **Undo**.
4. To fix many days at once, tap the pencil at the top of the month. Tap the days, then **Done** at the bottom.

![The day sheet for Friday, November 13: Period today is on and Heavy is picked. The switch is circled.](cycle-day)

**History** on the Cycle screen shows a bar for each cycle, your shortest and longest cycle, and **Patterns** counted from what you logged.

> Note: Days to come can't be logged.
