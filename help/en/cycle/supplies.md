A few days before your next period may start, the Cycle screen can ask: "Your period may start soon. Need anything?"

1. Tap **Add to a list**.
2. Tick what you need, or type your own.
3. Under **Save to**, pick one of your private shopping lists, or **New private list**.
4. Tap **Save**.

![The Supplies screen with Pads and Pain relief ticked and New private list picked. Save is circled.](cycle-supplies)

Only the items you ticked are added, to a list only you can see.

Tap × on the card to hide it until next time. To stop it, switch off **Suggest supplies before my period** in **Cycle settings**.
