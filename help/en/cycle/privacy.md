In Cycle, tap the gear for **Cycle settings**.

1. **Reminders**: **Period reminder** comes a few days before. **Ask if my period started** asks the day after the expected range. The lock screen only says "You have a reminder. Tap to open".
2. **Privacy**: **Lock Cycle** asks for Face ID, fingerprint or phone code. The app switcher hides Cycle, and Android blocks screenshots of it. **Show clear text on the lock screen** shows full reminders.
3. Also under **Privacy**, **Show on my calendar**: your phone's calendar shows a drop, filled if logged, outline if predicted. The family never sees it.
4. **Import (CSV)** brings back an **Export (CSV)** file, keeping the days already here.

![Cycle settings with your usual lengths, the Reminders with the supplies switch, and the Privacy section. Lock Cycle is circled.](cycle-settings)

Your data stays on this phone and its backup, never on daili's server. Keep iCloud Backup or Google backup on.

> Note: Signing out stops the reminders. Deleting your account erases the data on this phone. More in the privacy policy on daili.app.
