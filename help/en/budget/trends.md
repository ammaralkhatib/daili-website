For a budget that repeats, you can compare its months.

1. In the budget, tap **Compare months** under what's left, or **⋯** → **Compare months**.
2. Pick how many months to show at the top.
3. Tap a bar to see what was spent and the limit of that month.

![Compare months: six bars from May to October. August went over the limit. October's bar is circled.](budget-trends)

A bar in another colour went over that month's limit.

Below the chart, each category is compared with the month before, up to the same day. So in the middle of a month, you compare half a month with half a month.

> Note: A one-time budget, like a holiday, has nothing to compare, so it doesn't show **Compare months**.
