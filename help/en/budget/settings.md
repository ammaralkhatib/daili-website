In a budget, tap **⋯** → **Settings**. **Details** come first: name, icon, period, **Limit**, currency and **Who can see it**. Tap **Save** at the bottom.

![Budget settings with Move what's left to next month switched on. The switch is circled.](budget-settings)

**Move what's left to next month** is for repeating budgets with a limit. Money you don't spend is added to next month's limit; the budget then shows, for example, "of €2,500 + €212 from September". Overspending is never carried.

Under **More**: **Split costs**, **Categories**, **Export** and **Delete budget**.

In **Categories**, each row shows this month's spending, and **New category** is at the top. daili warns you if the category limits add up to more than the budget.

**Export** makes a CSV file for Excel. In German-style regions it uses semicolons and 12,50.

> Note: Anyone who sees a budget can export it. Only its maker, or a family admin who can see it, can change or delete it. A new currency changes the label, not the amounts.
