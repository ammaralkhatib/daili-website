Budget is free.

1. On Home, tap **Budget**. If a budget opens, tap its name at the top. Then tap **New budget**.
2. Pick **Household**, **Holiday**, **Party** or **Start empty**.
3. Choose the icon, the period and, if you like, a **Limit**. **Starts on** can be **Last day of the month**.
4. If others are in your family, pick under **Who can see it**: **Everyone in the family**, **Choose people** or **Only me**. Tap **Create budget**.

![A household budget for October: an overdue bill on top, what is left, the colour stripe and the categories. The Add expense button is circled.](budget-start)

Overdue bills sit on top, then what's left, about how much per day, and a colour stripe of the spending. No limit yet? Tap **Set a limit**.

Tap a category for what's left, last month, its entries and **Edit limit**.

![The Groceries sheet: £372.15 left of £600 and four entries. The Add to Groceries button is circled.](budget-category)

To switch budgets, tap the name at the top.

A repeating budget with a limit shows how last month went, for 7 days.

> Note: Offline, you see budgets you opened before but can't change them.
