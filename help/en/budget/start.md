Budget is free.

1. On Home, tap **Budget**, then **New budget**. Later, **New budget** is under the budget's name at the top.
2. Pick **Household month**, **Holiday**, **Party** or **Start empty**. Household repeats every month; Holiday and Party are one time.
3. Choose the **Currency** and, if you like, a **Limit**.
4. Under **Who can see it**, pick **Everyone in the family**, **Choose people** or **Only me**.
5. Tap **Create budget**.

![A budget for October with what is left, the balances and the categories. The + button is circled.](budget-start)

Tap **+** to add an entry: amount, category, **Title**, **Date**, **Paid by**, **Payment method**, **Repeat** and a **Note**. Each category can have its own limit: **⋯** → **Categories**.

Arrows show other months. Search, or filter by **Paid by**.

> Note: Only the person who made a budget can make it private. They, or a family admin who can see it, change its **Settings** or delete it. Anyone who sees it can **Export** a CSV file there. A new currency changes the label, not the amounts.
