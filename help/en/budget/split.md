Paying for things together? daili keeps count.

1. In a budget, tap **⋯** → **Settings**.
2. Under **More**, tap **Split costs** and switch it on.
3. Under **Who shares the costs**, keep at least two people. Tap **Save**.

Every **Shared cost** is split equally between them. Switch it off for an entry that only the payer covers.

The budget shows who owes whom, counting all months together. Tap **Settle up** for the details.

![The Split costs screen. Marco pays Lena £224.74. The Mark paid button is circled.](budget-split)

Under **Who pays whom**, tap **Mark paid**. Check the amount and the date, then tap **Mark paid** again. If you type more than the suggested amount, daili warns you. The payment is not counted as spending.

Past payments are under **Settle-up history**, and the top says when you last settled.

> Note: A budget that only you can see can't split costs.
