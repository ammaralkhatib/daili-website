Share costs, also with friends without daili.

1. In a budget, tap **⋯** → **Settings**.
2. Under **More**, tap **Split costs** and switch it on.
3. Under **Who shares the costs**, pick the people.
4. Under **Guests**, tap **Add guest** for a friend. Tap a guest to rename or delete them. Tap **Save**.

Every **Shared cost** is split equally. In **Paid by**, a guest can pay, and **For whom** picks who shares it.

**Settle up** shows who owes whom, all months together. Under **Who pays whom**, tap **Mark paid**. Past payments are under **Settle-up history**.

![The Split costs screen with a guest, marked Guest, and Who pays whom. The Share button at the top right is circled.](budget-split)

Tap **Share** at the top right. **Send summary** messages who pays whom. **Link for guests** is a read-only page, no app needed: the budget's creator or an admin taps **Create link**, then **Copy** or **Share**. **Stop sharing** turns it off.

![The Share with guests sheet: Send summary, and the link with Copy, Share and Stop sharing circled.](budget-share)

> Note: A budget only you can see splits only with guests. A guest still used in entries can't be deleted.
