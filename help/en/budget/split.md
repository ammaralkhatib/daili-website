Paying for things together? daili keeps count.

1. In a budget, tap **⋯** → **Settings**.
2. Under **Split costs**, tap **Split shared costs** and switch it on.
3. Under **Who shares the costs**, keep at least two people. Tap **Save**.

Every **Shared cost** is split equally between them. Switch it off for an entry that only the payer covers.

The **Balances** card on the budget shows who owes whom, counting all months together.

![The Split costs screen. Lena owes Marco £28.17. The Mark as paid button is circled.](budget-split)

To settle a debt, tap **Settle up**, then **Mark as paid** next to a payment, and **Mark as paid** again. The payment is not counted as spending.

> Note: A budget that only you can see can't split costs.
