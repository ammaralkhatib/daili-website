Rent, subscriptions and salary come back every month. **Repeating** shows them all in one place.

1. In a budget, tap **⋯** → **Repeating**.
2. At the top: about how much goes out each month, and how much comes in.
3. Tap a row for **Change from the next one**, **End this repeat** or **Show entries**.

**Change from the next one** opens the next entry. Pick **This and following** to change all later ones too.

**End this repeat** removes the entries after today. Paid bills stay.

To make something repeat, add it and pick **Repeat** under **More: repeat, bill, note, card or cash**.

![The entry editor for Rent, repeating Monthly. The If it falls on a weekend row, set to Friday before, is circled.](budget-repeating)

Monthly, every 3 months or yearly? Under **If it falls on a weekend**, pick **Friday before**, **Monday after** or **Keep the date**. If the date is on a weekend, a line shows where it moves. It works the same on app.daili.app.
