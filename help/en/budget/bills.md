A bill is an expense you still have to pay, like rent.

1. In a budget, tap **Add expense** and pick the due day at the top.
2. Under **More: repeat, bill, note, card or cash**, switch on **This is a bill** and pick a **Reminder**.

The reminder goes to the payer, or to everyone who sees the budget if it is **No one**. Bills to pay are under **Coming up**; overdue ones sit on top. Once you pay, tap **Mark paid**.

Bills also show on the calendar. To hide them, switch off **Bills** in **Calendar settings**.

Budget alerts come at 80 % and 100 % of the limit, counting money moved in from last month.

![The Notifications screen, with the Bill reminders and Budget alerts switches.](notifications-choose)

Turn these off in **Settings** → **Notifications**: **Bill reminders** and **Budget alerts**.

From a shopping list: **⋯** → **Add to budget**.

> Note: Bill reminders come even in quiet hours. Budget alerts wait until quiet hours end.
