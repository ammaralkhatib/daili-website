A bill is an expense you still have to pay, like rent.

1. In a budget, tap **+** and add the expense.
2. Switch on **This is a bill**. The **Date** is the day it is due.
3. Pick a **Reminder**, like **3 days before**.

The reminder goes to the person in **Paid by**, or to everyone who sees the budget if nobody is picked. Once you pay, tap **Mark paid**.

Bills show on the calendar on their due day; tap one to open its budget. To hide them, switch off **Bills** in **Calendar settings**.

A budget alert comes at 80 % and 100 % of the limit.

![The Notifications screen, with the Bill reminders and Budget alerts switches.](notifications-choose)

Turn these off in **Settings** → **Notifications**: **Bill reminders** and **Budget alerts**.

From a shopping list: **⋯** → **Add to budget**, or tap **Add** after the last item is ticked.

> Note: Bill reminders come even in quiet hours. Budget alerts wait until quiet hours end.
