1. In a budget, tap **Add expense**.
2. Type the amount. Your last category is already picked.
3. The day is at the top: **Today**, **Yesterday** or another day.
4. Tap the payer row to change who paid and, in a split budget, whether it is shared.
5. Tap the button with the amount to save.

![Adding a £34 expense with the number pad. The Today button at the top is circled.](budget-entry)

Tap **Income** at the top for money coming in. Repeat, bill and note are under **More: repeat, bill, note, card or cash**. Tap **Undo** right after saving to take it back.

On Home, tap **+** on the Budget tile, or Quick add → **Expense**.

To add an entry again, swipe it right, or open it and tap **Add again** under **More: repeat, bill, note, card or cash**. A copy dated today opens; save it.

> Note: An expense that couldn't be sent while offline is kept. daili offers it the next time you add one.
