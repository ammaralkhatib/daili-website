Never miss a birthday again. Everyone in your family sees the same list, with the next one at the top.

1. On Home, tap **Birthdays**, then **+**.
2. Pick **Birthday**, **Anniversary** or **Celebration**.
3. Type the **Name** and pick the **Month** and **Day**.
4. Know the year? Turn on **Show year** and pick the **Year**. daili then shows how old they turn.
5. Tap **Create**.

To change or delete one, open it and tap the menu button at the top.

> Note: A new birthday already has one reminder, on the day itself. Change it or add more before you tap **Create**.
