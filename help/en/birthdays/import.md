Your phone may already know your family's birthdays. Bring them in, in one go.

![The Birthday settings sheet. The Import from contacts row is circled.](birthdays-import)

1. On Home, tap **Birthdays**, then the gear at the top.
2. Tap **Import from contacts**, then **Continue**, and allow access to your contacts.
3. Tick the birthdays you want, or tap **Select all**.
4. Leave **Remind me on the day** on if you'd like a reminder.
5. Tap **Import**.

With no birthdays yet, you'll also find **Import from contacts** right on the Birthdays screen.

> Note: Your contacts stay on your phone. Only the birthdays you tick are uploaded: the name and the date, nothing else.
