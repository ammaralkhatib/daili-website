Each birthday can have up to 5 reminders. One a few days before gives you time to buy a present.

1. Open the birthday, tap the menu button at the top, then **Edit**.
2. Tap **Add reminder**.
3. Pick one, like the day before at 18:00. Or tap **Custom…** and choose **When** and the **Time**.
4. Tap **Save**.

To change the time new reminders start with, tap the gear on the **Birthdays** screen and set **Default reminder time**.

> Note: The default time only counts on this phone, and only for new reminders. Birthdays you already added keep their times.
