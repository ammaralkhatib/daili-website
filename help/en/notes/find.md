Notes has two tabs: **Mine** and **Shared with me**.

1. Type in **Search notes**. While you search, Type and Tag chips show under the field.
2. Or tap the filter button: **Type**, **Tags**, **Pinned only**. **New tag** makes a tag right there.
3. Tap **Done**. Each active filter shows as a chip. Tap its ✕ to clear it.

![The Filter sheet: Type, Tags with New tag, and Pinned only.](notes-filter)

A card shows the title, the date, the first attachment or one line of text, tag colour dots, a reminder and small icons for what the note holds. The top part hides while you scroll.

![Recently deleted. One note, gone in 27 days, with Delete forever and Restore.](notes-trash)

Right after deleting, tap **Undo**. Later: **⋯** → **Recently deleted** → **Restore**. Deleted notes wait 30 days, then they are gone for good.
