1. Open **Settings** → **Notes** → **Pictures and recordings**.
2. Pick **On this phone** or **Daili cloud**.

![Pictures and recordings, with On this phone picked and Daili cloud below it.](notes-files)

**On this phone** is free and the default. Files are only on this phone, and in its backup on iPhone. Others see a small chip instead.

**Daili cloud** is part of Daili Plus: 5 GB per family. Everyone the note is shared with sees the files, on every device.

Sharing a note with phone-only files asks: **Share text only** or **Move pictures to the cloud**. A file waiting to upload shows a cloud badge. Tap it to try again.

When Plus ends, nothing is deleted: cloud files stay, new ones stay on the phone (**Daili cloud (paused)**).

A drawing can be edited only on the phone it was made on. A cloud drawing needs a connection.

> Note: On Android, note pictures and recordings are not in the phone backup. Daili cloud keeps them safe.
