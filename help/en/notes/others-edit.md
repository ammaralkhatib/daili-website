A shared note can be changed by others too, if you let them.

1. In your note, tap **Change** at **Who can see it**, or the **Only me** chip while writing.
2. Pick **Whole family** or **Pick members**.
3. Switch on **Others can edit**.

![Who can see it with Whole family picked and Others can edit switched on. The switch is circled.](notes-others-edit)

They then see **Edit** in the note. They can change the title, text and link. They can add pictures if your family has **Daili cloud**. Only you can delete, pin, tag, set a reminder or change who sees it.

When someone else changed the note last, it says **Edited by** with their name and the time.

![The note Grandma's visit with the line Edited by Marco · Today under the title. The line is circled.](notes-edited-by)

Someone saved while you were editing? daili asks: **Use their version**, or **Keep mine as a new note** (a private copy).

> Note: Setting the note back to **Only me** switches **Others can edit** off. Nobody gets a notification about an edit.
