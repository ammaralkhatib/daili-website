daili can read your day to you, with your phone's own voice. Nothing is sent anywhere.

![Home with the Read aloud button, a talking face, next to the +. The Read aloud button is circled.](home-read-aloud)

1. On Home, tap the talking-face button next to **+**. You hear what **Today** shows: events, to-dos, habits, shopping, meals and birthdays.
2. Tap it again to stop. Leaving Home stops it too.
3. Press and hold it to open its settings.

In **Settings** → **Read aloud**, pick the **Speed** and the **Voice**, and tap **Hear a sample**. Switch off **Show button on Home** to hide the button.

![The Read aloud settings with Show button on Home, the speed and the voice. The Show button on Home switch is circled.](settings-read-aloud)

Sounds robotic? Get a nicer voice for free. iPhone: open the **Settings** app → **Accessibility** → **Read & Speak** → **Voices** → your language → **Voice**. Download a voice marked **(premium)** or **(enhanced)**, on Wi-Fi. Android: when **Show me how** shows on Home or in these settings, tap it, then **Install voice**, and download your language. daili uses the new voice by itself.
