You need an account to use daili.

![The Sign in screen with Google and Apple at the top. The Sign up bar at the bottom is circled.](start-sign-in)

1. Open daili and tap **Get started**. A new phone opens **Create account**.
2. The bar at the bottom switches screens: **Sign up** or **Log in**.
3. Tap **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple** at the top. Or, under **or with email**, type your email and password and tap **Sign in**.
4. New with email? Fill in your name, email and a password twice, then tap **Create account**.
5. Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?**, type your email and tap **Send reset link**.

![The Create account screen. The Continue with Google and Continue with Apple buttons are circled.](start-register)

Your password needs at least 8 characters. Next, you add your picture and name, and create or join a family.

By continuing, you agree to the **Terms of use**; the **Privacy policy** explains how daili handles your data.

> Note: **Continue with Apple** is only on iPhone. On Android, use Google or your email. After you reinstall daili, you sign in again.
