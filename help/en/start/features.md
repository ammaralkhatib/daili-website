Right after you create or join your first family, daili asks **What do you want to use Daili for?** Every card starts ticked.

1. Tap a card to read about it.
2. Tap a card's box to turn it off, or on again.
3. Tap **Continue**. Or tap **Skip** to keep everything on.

Calendar and Family are not on the list; they stay on Home. You can change your choice any time in **Settings** under **Home screen**.

> Note: The Cycle card only puts a tile on Home. Cycle itself starts when you tap the tile and finish its short setup. It is private: it stays only on this phone, and nobody in your family can see it. Not for you? Untick the card, or hide the tile later.
