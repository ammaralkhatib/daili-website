At the top of **Today**, tap **Use my location** or **Choose a city**.

1. Tap the card for the **Weather** screen.
2. See the next 24 hours and **10 days**. Tap a day for its hours.
3. Scroll down for wind, UV, humidity, sunrise and sunset.

![The Weather screen with the sky, the next hours and 10 days. Today's row is circled.](start-weather)

On Home, the weather at the top opens the **Weather** screen. The **Weather** tile says when rain is coming. To hide it: **⋯** → **Hide weather**, or **Weather on Today** in **Settings**.

In **Calendar**, the sheet of today or a coming day shows the weather top right. On Home, tap the week card's next event for hourly weather.

![Today's day sheet with the weather at the top right (16° / 7°). The weather is circled.](calendar-day-weather)

![The Home week popup with today's weather hour by hour, above today's events. The hourly weather is circled.](start-week-weather)

**Weather in daily summary** (**Calendar settings** → **Daily summary**) adds the weather to your morning and evening summary. daili keeps a rough place (about 10 km) until you switch it off.

> Note: Your location is approximate and only used for the weather. More in the privacy policy on daili.app.
