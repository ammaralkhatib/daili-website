At the top of **Today**, tap **Use my location** or **Choose a city**. With **Location is off**, choose a city.

1. Tap the card for the **Weather** screen. Its sky follows the weather and time of day.
2. See the next 24 hours and **10 days**. Tap a day for its hours.
3. Today's events show their weather. Scroll down for wind, UV, humidity, sunrise and sunset.

![The Weather screen with the sky, the next hours and 10 days. Today's row is circled.](start-weather)

On Home, **Today** shows the sky and the temperature. The **Weather** tile says when rain is coming. To hide it: **⋯** → **Hide weather**, or **Weather on Today** in **Settings**.

**Weather in daily summary** (**Calendar settings** → **Daily summary**) adds one weather line to your morning and evening summary. daili keeps a rough place (about 10 km) until you switch it off.

> Note: Your location is approximate and only used for the weather. The bottom line names the source. More in the privacy policy on daili.app.
