At the top of Home, your picture opens **Settings** and the bold line switches family or group. On the right: the weather, a calendar icon for **Today** and the bell for **Activity**.

1. The top card lists today's next events, up to 3, with start and end times. Nothing left today? It shows the next one. Linked and phone calendars count too. Tap it for the whole week.
2. Each tile opens one area, like **Shopping** or **Notes**, with a live fact like tonight's dinner. A tile's small round button shows a quick summary.
3. Tap the round **+** at the bottom right to add an event, a list, a note and more. Next to it: **Read aloud**.

A new account also gets a **Get started with daili** checklist. **Hide this list** removes it for good.

![Home with Hi, Lena and The Bergers at the top. The week card lists today's Dentist, Piano lesson and Soccer practice with times and +1 more, and is circled.](start-home)

In the week, swipe the events sideways for another day, and the day strip for another week.
