Home is where daili opens.

1. The top card lists today's next events, up to 3, with start and end times. Nothing left today? It shows the next one. Linked and phone calendars count too. Tap it for the whole week.
2. Each tile opens one area, like **Shopping** or **Notes**. A tile's small arrow shows a quick summary.
3. Tap the round **+** button at the bottom right to add an event, a shopping list or a to-do list.

**Today** at the top right shows the sky and temperature. The **Weather** tile says when rain is coming.

A new account also gets a **Get started with daili** checklist. **Hide this list** removes it for good.

![Home with the week strip. The week card lists today's Dentist, Piano lesson and Soccer practice with times and +1 more, and is circled.](start-home)

In the week, swipe the events sideways for another day, and the day strip for another week.

> Note: Tap your picture at the top of Home to open **Settings**.
