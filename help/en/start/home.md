Home is where daili opens. It shows what's coming up and gets you anywhere in one tap.

1. The card at the top shows your family's next event. Tap it to see the whole week and add an event.
2. Each tile opens one area, like **Shopping** or **Notes**. The small arrow on a tile shows a quick summary.
3. Tap the round **+** button at the bottom right to add an event, a shopping list or a to-do list.

A new account also gets a **Get started with daili** card: invite someone, add an event, turn on notifications and more. It ticks itself off as you go. **Hide this list** removes it for good.

![The Home screen with the week card and the tiles](start-home)

> Note: Tap your picture at the top of Home to open **Settings**.
