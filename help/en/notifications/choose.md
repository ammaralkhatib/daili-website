You choose what daili tells you.

![Notification settings. The Someone is going shopping switch, under Family changes (what members do), is circled.](notifications-choose)

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Notifications**.
2. Under **Reminders (for you)**, switch on the reminders you want, like **Event reminders**, **Birthday reminders**, **Bill reminders** or **Budget alerts**.
3. Under **Family changes (what members do)**, pick the areas where you want to hear what others do, like **Shopping** or **Calendar**.
4. Switch on **Quiet hours**. It starts with 21:00 to 07:00. Tap **From** or **Until** to change the times.

**Someone is going shopping**: someone tapped **I'm going shopping**. **Your meal wishes**: someone planned your wish. Rewards come under **Habit news**.

During quiet hours, family changes wait until they end, except **Someone is going shopping**. Habit notifications are held back too. Reminders for events, to-dos and birthdays still come through.

Your daily summary is in **Calendar settings** (the gear in **Calendar**) under **Daily summary**.

> Note: These switches belong to your account. Everyone in the family chooses their own.
