Too many notifications, or too few? You choose what daili tells you.

![The Notifications screen. The Reminders (for you) group with its seven switches is circled.](notifications-choose)

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Notifications**.
2. Under **Reminders (for you)**, switch on the reminders you want, like **Event reminders**, **Birthday reminders**, **Bill reminders** or **Budget alerts**.
3. Under **Family changes (what members do)**, pick the areas where you want to hear what others do, like **Shopping** or **Calendar**.
4. Switch on **Quiet hours**. It starts with 21:00 to 07:00. Tap **From** or **Until** to change the times.

During quiet hours, family changes wait and arrive together when quiet hours end. Habit notifications are held back too. Reminders for events, to-dos and birthdays still come through.

The daily summary of your events is in **Calendar settings** under **Daily summary**: **Morning**, **Evening** and **Weather in daily summary**. Open them from the gear in **Calendar**.

> Note: These switches belong to your account. Everyone in the family chooses their own.
