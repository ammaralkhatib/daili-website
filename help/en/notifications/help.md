A reminder didn't come? Check these, in this order.

1. **Your phone allows daili.** Open **Settings**, then **Notifications**. If a note at the top says notifications are off, tap its button.
2. **The switch is on.** On the same screen, check the switch for that kind, like **To-do reminders**.
3. **Quiet hours.** Family changes and habit notifications wait while **Quiet hours** are on. Other reminders don't.
4. **Focus or Do Not Disturb.** Your phone may be holding notifications back. Check its Focus (iPhone) or Do Not Disturb (Android).
5. **Battery saving (Android).** Some phones stop apps in the background. In your phone's settings, let daili run without battery limits.

> Note: A to-do only reminds you when it has a due date and a **Reminder**.
