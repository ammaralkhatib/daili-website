On Android, daili can say your event and to-do reminders out loud, like "Dentist starts in 10 minutes." It's off until you turn it on. iPhone doesn't have it.

1. Open **Settings** → **Read aloud**. Or, on the **Notifications** screen, tap **Speak my reminders**.
2. Under **Reminders**, switch on **Speak my reminders**. You hear a sample once.

It never speaks while your phone is on silent, vibrate or Do Not Disturb, during your quiet hours, or while daili's notifications are off.

It uses the voice and speed you picked for Read aloud, even with its Home button hidden. Other notifications are never spoken.

> Note: It also says the titles of your private events and to-dos. Anyone near your phone can hear them.
