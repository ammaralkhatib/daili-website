Your family sees everything not private: calendar, shopping, to-dos, birthdays, recipes, meal plan and habits.

When you add, change or delete something shared, the others get a notification with your name and its title: **Calendar**, **Shopping**, **To-dos**, **Birthdays & celebrations**, **Recipes** or **Meal plan**. **Family**: new members. **Habit news**: things like new shared habits, milestones and cheers. Quick follow-up changes arrive bundled.

Everyone chooses their own under **Settings** → **Notifications** → **Family changes (what members do)**, and can set **Quiet hours**.

An event for chosen people with a login notifies only them. A to-do assigned to someone also tells that person (**Assigned to you**).

The bell on Home shows who changed what. A shared note appears there, without a notification.

> Note: Never sent to your family: **Only me** items, **Cycle**, **Documents**, **Photos** and **Budget** entries. **Bill reminders** and **Budget alerts** go only to people who can see that budget.
