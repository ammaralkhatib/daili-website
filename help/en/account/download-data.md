You can ask for a copy of your data at any time. Your email address has to be verified first, because that's where the copy goes.

![The Profile screen. Download my data, under Account, is circled.](account-download-data)

1. Open **Settings**, then tap your name at the top.
2. Under **Account**, tap **Download my data**.
3. daili says "We'll email you when your export is ready." Watch your inbox.

Not verified yet? daili says so and offers **Send link**. Tap the link in the email, then try again.

> Note: The copy has what's stored on daili's servers. Your documents and photos are only on your phone, so they aren't in it.
