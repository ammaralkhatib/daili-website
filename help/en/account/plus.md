Every family gets 3 free AI actions a month. Plus gives 30, shared by the family, and 5 GB of Daili cloud. Months are calendar months.

![The Daili Plus sheet: what you get, the yearly price and the Get Plus button.](plus-sheet)

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Daili Plus**. The sheet also opens when free actions run out.
2. Tap **Get Plus**. Plus costs €9.99 a year in the euro area; the app shows the price in your store's currency. No trial.
3. On a new phone, tap **Restore purchases**.

Plus belongs to the family open when you buy; one subscription per store account. Settings shows **Active** or **Bought by a family member**, and AI actions left.

Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions; deleting the app or account does not cancel. When Plus ends, nothing is deleted: cloud files stay readable, new ones wait until Plus is back.

> Note: **Founder** families have Plus free (fair use: 50 AI actions a month). Details: Terms of Use on daili.app.
