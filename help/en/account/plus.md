Every family gets 3 AI actions a month free. Daili Plus gives 30, shared by the whole family, and 5 GB of Daili cloud. Months are calendar months.

![The Daili Plus sheet: what you get, the yearly price and the Get Plus button.](plus-sheet)

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Daili Plus**. The sheet also opens when the month's free actions are used up.
2. Tap **Get Plus**. You pay yearly, in your store's currency. No trial.
3. On a new phone, tap **Restore purchases**.

Plus belongs to the family open when you buy; one subscription per store account. Settings shows **Active** or **Bought by a family member**, and the AI actions left.

Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions; deleting the app or your account does not cancel. When Plus ends, nothing is deleted: cloud files stay readable, new ones wait until Plus is back.

> Note: **Founder** families have Plus free (fair use: 50 AI actions a month). Details: the Terms of Use on daili.app.
