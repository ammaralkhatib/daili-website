When you sign up with email, daili sends you a link to check it's yours. Until you confirm, some things stay locked: inviting someone by email, giving a child their own login, searching recipes online and downloading your data.

![The top of Settings. The Email not verified card with its Resend button is circled.](account-verify-email)

1. Tap the link in daili's email, then **Confirm address** on the page it opens. The link works for 3 days.
2. No email? Look in your spam folder.
3. Still nothing? Open **Settings**. On the **Email not verified** card, tap **Resend**.

Wrong address? Open **Settings**, tap your name, then **Change email address**. Your old address gets a notice, your other devices are signed out, and the newsletter moves too.

After two resends or a bounce, **Didn't get the email?** gets you personal help within a day.

Signed in with Google or Apple, or using an iCloud address? It's already verified.

> Note: You don't need a verified email to join a family with a code.
