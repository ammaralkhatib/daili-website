When you sign up with an email address, daili sends you a link to check it's really yours. Until you tap it, three things stay locked: inviting someone by email, giving a child their own login, and searching recipes online.

![The top of Settings. The Email not verified card with its Resend button is circled.](account-verify-email)

1. Tap the link in daili's email. It works for 3 days; an expired link offers you a fresh one.
2. No email? Look in your spam folder.
3. Still nothing? Open **Settings**. On the **Email not verified** card, tap **Resend**. The button then waits a minute.
4. Back in daili, your address shows **Verified**.

Couldn't the email be delivered? daili says so, and **Change email address** fixes it right there. After two resends or a bounce, **Didn't get the email?** gets you personal help within a day.

Signed in with Google or Apple? Then your email is already verified.

> Note: You don't need a verified email to join a family with a code.
