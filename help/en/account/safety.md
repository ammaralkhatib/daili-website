**Family only.** Only your family sees your data, plus links, screens or apps you add. Private items (**Only me**, **Private event**) stay hidden from family.

**Protected.** App, web app and website use only HTTPS. Passwords are stored as a one-way hash nobody can read.

**In the EU.** Our server is at Host Europe, under the GDPR.

**On your phone.** **Cycle**, **Documents**, **Photos** and phone calendars reach us only if you copy something into daili.

**No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.** Built-in AI (adults only) sends data to Google (Gemini) only when you start it, never for training.

**Your control.** **Download my data** and **Delete account** are in your profile. We review security regularly and fix problems right away.

> Note: daili isn't end-to-end encrypted. Our server reads your items for reminders and sync. Only daili's developer has admin access.

Found a security problem? Email support@daili.app with "Security" in the subject.
