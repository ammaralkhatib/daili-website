With **Help improve Daili** on, daili counts which screens you open and sends crash reports. That shows us what to fix and make better.

![Settings → About. The Help improve Daili switch is circled.](account-help-improve)

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **About**, below **Privacy policy**, switch **Help improve Daili** on or off.

The switch in Settings shows for admins, or once it's on. daili also asks on the last sign-up screen, and once on Home.

It counts numbers per day, never what you type, and nothing from Cycle. Off stops everything and deletes the stored counts.

Only adults can switch it on: an admin, or someone 18 or older.

> Note: The **Privacy policy** under **About** explains it in full.
