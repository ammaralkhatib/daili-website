![The Change picture sheet. Create avatar with AI is circled. Under Choose an avatar, Your AI avatars shows three versions.](account-avatar-ai)

1. In your profile, tap **Change picture**, then **Create avatar with AI**. For a child, admins use ⋮ in **Family** → **Change picture**.
2. **From a selfie**: tap **Take a selfie** or **Choose a photo**. Only you, facing the camera, in good light. Adults only; daili asks once if you're 18 or older.
3. **Pick your look**: pick age, skin tone, hair and more, then tap **Create avatar**. Children only get this: we never use their photo.
4. You get 3 versions. Tap your favourite, then **Use this avatar**.

![Pick your look with Adult, a skin tone, Long wavy and dark brown hair picked. Create avatar is circled.](account-avatar-ai-look)

All versions stay on this phone only (newest 30 per person), under **Your AI avatars**. Tap one to reuse it, free. Press and hold to save, share or delete.

> Note: One try uses one AI action: 3 a month are free, Daili Plus gives 30. Up to 10 avatars per family a month. Your selfie goes to our AI partner and is deleted right after.
