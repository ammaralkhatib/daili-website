Tap your picture at the top of Home to open **Settings**. You find these under **Preferences**.

![The Settings screen. The Preferences card with Language, Time format, Appearance, Vibration and Temperature unit is circled.](account-preferences)

1. **Language**: pick one of daili's languages, or **System default** to follow your phone.
2. **Time format**: **24-hour**, **12-hour**, or **System** to follow your phone.
3. **Appearance**: **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**. Under **App color**, pick **daili green** or **My color**. It tints buttons, links and cards.
4. **Vibration**: a buzz when something is done, saved or deleted.
5. **Weather on Today**: the weather card at the top of **Today**.
6. **Temperature unit**: **Automatic** follows your phone's region, or pick °C or °F. Wind and rain change with it.
7. **AI features (Google Gemini)**: lets Google's AI read your photos, video links and meal wishes. Off? daili asks again next time.

![The Preferences card. The Temperature unit row, set to Automatic, is circled.](account-temperature)

![The Appearance sheet. App color, with daili green and My color, is circled.](account-app-colour)

Except the language, these settings are for this phone only. Your family keeps their own.

> Note: The language you pick is also the language of daili's emails and reminders to you.
