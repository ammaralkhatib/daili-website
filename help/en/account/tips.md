Now and then, daili shows a tip: a small card at the bottom of the screen. It comes at most every 3 days, and only about things you haven't used yet.

1. To stop them, tap **Turn off tips** on the card.
2. Or open **Settings** and switch off **Show tips** under **Help**.
3. To get them back, switch **Show tips** on again.

Every guide is in the Help center anyway, tips or not.
