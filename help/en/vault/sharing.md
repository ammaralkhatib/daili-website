Shared Documents folders and Photos albums go onto every family phone.

1. Tap **⋮** on a folder or album, then **Share with …**. Or turn on **Share with family** under **Edit**.
2. Check who gets it, tap **Share with family**.

Everyone with a login gets it on all their phones. Children without one don't.

Anyone can add, delete and rename files. **Put back** in **Recently deleted** works for 30 days.

> Note: Files are locked on your phone (end-to-end encrypted). Our server only passes them on, can't open them, and deletes them once every phone has them (30 days at most). Each file: up to 25 MB.

Files only move while daili is open. "Waiting for a family phone to open Daili" means just that. A new phone waits too.

**Stop sharing** (anyone can): everyone keeps what they have, as a private folder or album. If you leave the family, you keep a copy.
