Keep family photos sorted in albums, right in daili.

1. On Home, tap **Photos**, then tap an album.
2. For a new album, tap the button at the bottom right (**New album**). Type an **Album name** and tap **Save**.
3. In an album, tap **+** and choose **From camera roll** or **Take photo**.
4. Tap a photo to see it big. Swipe to see the next one. The buttons at the top **Share** or **Delete** it.
5. Tap **All photos** to see the photos from every album together.

To rename or delete an album, press and hold it.

> Note: daili keeps its own copy of each photo. Deleting it here doesn't delete the original in your camera roll, and deleting the original doesn't delete it here.
