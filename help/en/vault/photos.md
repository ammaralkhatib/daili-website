Keep family photos sorted in albums.

![Photos with album cards and the privacy line under them. The ⋮ button on the first album is circled.](vault-photos)

1. On Home, tap **Photos**, then tap an album.
2. For a new album, tap the button at the bottom right (**New album**). Type an **Album name** and tap **Save**.
3. In an album, tap **+** and choose **From camera roll** or **Take photo**.
4. Tap a photo to see it big. Swipe to see the next one. The buttons at the top **Share** or **Delete** it.
5. **All photos** shows the photos of every album together.

To change or delete an album, tap its **⋮**, then **Edit** or **Delete**.

In two or more families or groups, **Show in** picks **All my families** or one family. A small tag marks albums shown only in this one.

As the line under the albums says, they are only on this phone, and only you see them.

> Note: daili keeps its own copy of each photo, so deleting one never deletes the other.
