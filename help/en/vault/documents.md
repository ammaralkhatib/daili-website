Keep insurance papers, school letters and receipts in folders.

![Documents with folder cards and the privacy line under them. The ⋮ button on the first folder is circled.](vault-documents)

1. On Home, tap **Documents**, then tap a folder.
2. For a new folder, tap the folder button at the bottom right (**New folder**). Type a **Folder name**, pick a **Color** and tap **Save**.
3. In a folder, tap **+** and choose **File**, **From camera roll** or **Take photo**.
4. Tap a file to open it. Tap **⋮** next to it to **Rename**, **Move**, **Share** or **Delete** it.
5. To change or delete a folder, tap its **⋮**, then **Edit** or **Delete**.

In two or more families or groups, **Show in** picks **All my families** or one family. A small tag marks folders shown only in this one.

**Share** sends a copy to another app, like email or a messenger. daili keeps its own copy, so deleting one never deletes the other.

> Note: Deleting a folder deletes the files inside it too. This can't be undone.
