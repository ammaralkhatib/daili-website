Your family can keep its own habit list: categories you name, and habits you saved to start again.

1. On **Habits**, tap **+**.
2. In **Start a habit**, tap the **Edit** chip in the row of categories.
3. Under **Your family's categories**, type a name and tap +. Use the buttons to rename, move or delete one.
4. Under **Daili's categories**, tap the eye to hide one you don't need.

![The Categories screen with the Morning routine category. The New category field is circled.](habits-categories)

To save a habit, switch on **Save to my habit list** in its form and pick a **Category**. It then shows in **Start a habit**. Press and hold it there to **Edit**, **Move to category** or **Delete**.

> Note: Your whole family sees these. The person who made one, or an admin, can change it. Only admins can hide daili's categories.
