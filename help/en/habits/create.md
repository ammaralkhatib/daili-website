![The New habit form with Some days, Tuesday and Repeat: Every 2 weeks. The Repeat row is circled.](habits-create)

1. On Home, tap **Habits**, then **+** at the bottom right.
2. Tap a habit, or **Make your own** at the bottom. **Added** marks ones you have.
3. Type a **Name**. Under **Icon**, tap a suggestion or **All**. Pick from 24 colours under **Colour**.
4. Pick who does it. **Each of us** starts with you picked.
5. Pick how often, and how you track it.
6. Tap **Start habit**. With **Save to my habit list** on, you can start it again later.

![The Choose an icon sheet with its groups. The search field is circled.](habits-icons)

**Some days** starts with only today on. To repeat every 2 to 8 weeks, tap **+** at **Repeat**, then pick the **First time** (when you edit: **Next time**). It then reads "Every 2 weeks · Tue".

To change a habit, open it and tap the pencil. Removing a person asks first: their ticks are deleted.

> Note: Only the person who made a habit can change it. An admin can also change shared ones.
