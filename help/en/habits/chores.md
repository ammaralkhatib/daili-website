Chores, like the dishes, show on **Habits** below the habits. Only admins (parents) add and change them.

![Emma's Habits page with the Chores section: Do the dishes (Emma's turn today), Take out the trash (first one gets it), Tidy your room. The Chores heading is circled.](habits-chores)

1. On **Habits**, tap **+**, then **Chore**.
2. Tap a suggestion or type a name. Pick the **Stars**.
3. Under **Who does it?**, pick people, in turn order.
4. With two or more people, under **How is it shared?** pick **Everyone does it**, **Take turns** (one a day; weekly for **A few times a week**) or **First one gets it**.
5. Under **When?**, pick **Every day**, **Some days**, **A few times a week** or **Just once**.
6. Tap **Add chore**.

![What do you want to add? with Habit and Chore. Chore is circled.](habits-new-chooser)

![The New chore form for Emma and Noah. How is it shared? with Take turns picked and the turn order. The card is circled.](habits-chore-form)

![The New chore form with First one gets it and When: Just once, Day: Today. The When card is circled.](habits-chore-form-once)

Or tap **No chores yet — add one**.

![The Habits screen with No chores yet — add one under Chores. The card is circled.](habits-chores-empty)

To change one, open it and tap the pencil. In **Edit chore**, the menu has **Archive** (keeps its history) and **Delete** (removes every tick).

> Note: Each person ticks their own; with **Take turns**, only whoever's turn it is. An admin can tick for a child without a login.
