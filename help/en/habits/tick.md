Tick a habit the moment you've done it.

![The Habits screen. The round tick button next to Stretch is circled.](habits-tick)

1. On Home, tap **Habits**, then the round button at the right of a habit. 🎉 means it's done. Unticked one by mistake? Tap **Undo**.
2. A counter fills a piece with each tap, even past its goal. To fix a mistake, press and hold it and tap the minus.
3. Forgot yesterday? Open the habit and tap ⋯, or hold a yes/no habit, then **Fill in yesterday**.
4. To fix an earlier day, open the habit and tap the day. You can change the last 7 days.

You tick your own habits and any **Together** habit. An admin can also tick for a child without a login.

The **Family** tab shows one bar with a part for each person. **Together** habits have their own row and count for whoever ticked.

Habits that aren't due today wait under **Coming up**.

> Note: Nobody can tick for another adult.
