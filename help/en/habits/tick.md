Tick a habit the moment you've done it. Each tick gives the family energy.

![The Habits screen. The round tick button next to Stretch is circled.](habits-tick)

1. On Home, tap **Habits**.
2. Tap the round button at the right of a habit. For a counter, tap **One more** until you reach the target.
3. Forgot yesterday? Press and hold the habit, then tap **Fill in yesterday**.
4. To fix an earlier day, tap the habit's row and then the day. You can change the last 7 days.

You tick your own habits and any **Together** habit. An admin can also tick for a child without a login. The row then says **You tick for** and the child's name.

> Note: Nobody can tick for another adult. Everyone with a login ticks their own habits.
