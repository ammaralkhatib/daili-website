Every finished habit day gives one star. Stars fill rewards, like a cinema trip.

![The Rewards tab with the family's "Day trip to the zoo" reward, its stars split by person. The family reward card is circled.](habits-rewards)

1. On **Habits**, tap **Rewards** at the bottom, then **+**.
2. Pick an idea. Under **For**, pick **Family** or **You**. An admin can pick anyone.
3. Set the **Stars needed**, from 5 to 500.
4. The row below sets **Which habits count** and **Starts again when done**. Tap **Save reward**.

![The New reward form with the Game night idea picked. The Stars needed row is circled.](habits-reward-new)

A reward only counts stars earned after it was made. One star fills every reward it counts for. Each person earns up to 5 stars a day. A counter earns its star at its goal. Rest days and private habits earn none. **Together** habits fill family rewards only.

![Right after a tick, the "+1 star for you" message shows the two rewards the star went into. The message is circled.](habits-star-toast)

When a reward is full, the family gets a notification. **Plan it** opens a new all-day event for next Saturday. Once you've enjoyed it, an admin taps **We did it**.

![The "You reached a reward" sheet for Eat out together. The Plan it in the calendar button is circled.](habits-reward-reached)

> Note: On the web, rewards are the **Rewards** tab in **Habits**.
