Spend stars from habits and chores on rewards, like a cinema trip.

![Emma's Rewards page: 19 stars to spend, the family reward, Asked — waiting on Ice cream outing, and Cinema trip with Ask for it. Ask for it is circled.](habits-rewards)

1. On **Habits**, tap **Rewards** at the bottom, then **+**.
2. Pick an idea. Under **For**, pick **Family** or **You**. An admin can pick anyone.
3. Set the **Price in stars** (one person) or the **Stars needed** (family), from 5 to 500. Tap **Save reward**.

![The New reward form with Cinema trip for Emma. The Price in stars row is circled.](habits-reward-new)

The top card shows stars to spend. Enough for your reward? Tap **Ask for it**. The stars are set aside until a parent answers. **Take back** cancels.

![The Rewards tab for a parent. Needs your answer: Emma asks for Ice cream outing (25 stars), with Not now and Approve. The card is circled.](habits-reward-answer)

Parents answer under **Needs your answer**: **Approve** spends the stars, **Not now** gives them back. Admins can also **Give it now**. With **A parent says yes first** off, asking spends them at once.

A family reward fills with the stars everyone earns after it was made. Full? **Plan it** adds an event; afterwards an admin taps **We did it**.

![Right after a tick, the +1 star for you message with the bunny shows the two rewards the star went into. The message is circled.](habits-star-toast)

![The "You reached a reward" sheet for Eat out together. The Plan it in the calendar button is circled.](habits-reward-reached)

> Note: On the web, rewards are the **Rewards** tab in **Habits**.
