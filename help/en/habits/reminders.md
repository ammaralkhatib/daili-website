A nudge at the right time makes a habit easier to keep.

1. Start a new habit, or open one and tap the pencil.
2. Under **More**, turn on **Reminder** and pick a time.
3. Tap **Start habit**, or **Save** if you're changing one.

When the reminder comes, tap **Done** to tick the habit without opening daili. A counter needs daili to open, so you can count.

In an **Each of us** habit, everyone starts with the same time. Later, each person can change their own.

> Note: **Together** habits don't have reminders.
