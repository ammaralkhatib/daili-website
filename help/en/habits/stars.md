Each finished habit day earns the stars set in the habit's **Stars** row: 0, 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10. A chore earns its own stars.

1. Stars count from up to 5 habits a day per person. Chores come on top.
2. A counter earns its stars at its goal. Rest days and private habits earn none.
3. **Together** habits fill family rewards only.

A buddy is an animal that cheers with you. Tap **Your buddy** on **Rewards**, or, once picked, your buddy next to **Today**. Pick one of 12 animals, or **No buddy, just stars**.

![The Pick your buddy sheet with twelve animals and No buddy, just stars.](habits-buddy)

Your buddy shows next to **Today**, in the star message after a tick and on the reward sheets. When every habit and chore due today is done, it cheers in **All done for today!**. Tap **Yay!** to close it.

![The All done for today! card with the bunny, 4 done · +2 stars today and Yay!](habits-all-done)

> Note: Everyone picks their own buddy. An admin picks one for a child without a login.
