A streak counts the days in a row you did a habit. For a habit you do a few times a week, it counts weeks.

![A habit's own page with its year grid and a month calendar. The chips with the streak, Best 19 and Every day are circled.](habits-streaks)

1. Open a habit to see your **Current streak** and your **Best**.
2. Busy day? Open a habit and tap ⋯, or hold a yes/no habit, then tap **Rest day today**. It covers all your habits today.
3. Going away? In the same menu, tap **Pause for a few days** and pick the last day of the break.

> Note: You get one rest day a week, and a pause lasts up to 14 days. Both keep your streaks safe, but they earn no stars.
