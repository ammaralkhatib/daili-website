Minzi is your family's cat. She lives in a calm room with an armchair on the **Habits** screen. She naps, kneads, chases her tail, watches a butterfly and plays with a yarn ball. Tap her and see what she does.

![The Habits screen with Minzi in her room. The Family energy bar is circled.](habits-minzi)

1. Tick your habits. Each tick adds to the **Family energy** bar.
2. When the bar is full, Minzi sets off on a trip that night.
3. In the morning she's back. Tap the card to open her postcard.
4. Tap her name at the bottom to see her **Postcards**, trips and family streak.

To give the cat another name, an admin taps **Rename** on that screen. The energy line and the tab then use the new name.

Minzi also shows up outside **Habits**, but only when you finish a whole list or all of Today's to-dos.

> Note: Each person earns energy once per habit per day. Private habits and rest days give no energy.
