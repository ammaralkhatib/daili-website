![An event opened from the calendar, with chips under Add. The pencil (Edit event) is circled, with the trash (Delete) next to it.](calendar-edit-delete)

1. In **Calendar**, tap the event.
2. Tap the pencil, make your changes and tap **Save**.
3. Or tap the trash, then **Delete** to confirm.

Missing a place or notes? Tap it under **Add**. **Reminder** there sets only yours.

For a repeating event, daili asks after **Save** what your change applies to: **This event only**, **This and following events** or **All events**. The trash offers the same three right away.

To move an event, hold it and drag it. **Month**: to another day. **Week** and **3 days**: to another day and time. **Day**: to another time. A repeating event asks **This event only** or **This and following events**. **Undo** in the message puts it back, except after **This and following events**.

> Note: Anyone in the family can change or delete a shared event, and the activity list shows who did it. A private event can only be changed by the person who made it.
