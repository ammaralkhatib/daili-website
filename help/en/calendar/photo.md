Dates on paper? The scan button in **New event** reads them.

![The New event screen. The scan button at the right of the Title field is circled.](calendar-photo)

1. Open **Calendar**, tap **+**, then the scan button at the right of **Title**.
2. Tap **Take photo** or **Choose from library**.
3. For a library photo, tap **Read this photo**.
4. One date: the form is filled. Check it and tap **Save**.
5. Several: untick what you don't need, then tap **Add N events**.

A work roster? Tap **Shift roster** and type your name as written on it. Only your shifts are read, up to two months.

Printed text works best, handwriting less so. For a PDF, photograph the page.

> Note: AI is for adults (18+). The photo goes to Google's AI to be read, then is deleted, without camera data like location. **Settings** → **AI features (Google Gemini)** switches it off.

> Note: A photo read uses one AI action. Every family gets 3 AI actions a month free; Daili Plus gives 30.
