Have the dates on paper? The scan icon in **New event** reads them.

![The New event screen. The scan icon at the top right is circled.](calendar-photo)

1. Open **Calendar**, tap **+**, then the scan icon at the top right.
2. Tap **Take photo** or **Choose from library**.
3. A library photo is shown first. If the dates are readable, tap **Read this photo**.
4. One date found: the form is filled. Check it and tap **Save**.
5. Several: untick what you don't need, tap a date or time to change it, then tap **Add N events**. Past dates start unticked.

Printed text works best, handwriting less so. PDFs don't work yet — photograph the page.

> Note: The photo goes to Google's AI to be read, then is deleted. daili never stores it, and removes camera data like location first.

> Note: Every family gets a few free photo reads each month, shared with video reads. Daili Plus has more — and if your family joined early, reads are free for life.
