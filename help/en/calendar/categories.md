An event's colour comes from its category. So "Sport" looks the same everywhere. Every family starts with six: Family, Work, School, Sport, Health and Free time.

![The Event categories screen with six categories. The Add category row is circled.](calendar-categories)

1. On Home, tap **Family**.
2. Under **Calendar**, tap **Event categories**.
3. Tap **Add category**, type a **Name**, pick a **Color** and tap **Add**.
4. Tap a category to rename it or change its colour. Tap **⋮** to delete it.

To give an event a category, tap **Category** in the event. In **Calendar**, tap **Filter** to see just one category.

> Note: Categories are shared with the whole family, and anyone can change them. A family can have up to 30.
