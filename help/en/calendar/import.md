Got a calendar file (.ics) from a school or club? daili copies its events into your family calendar once.

1. Open **Calendar** and tap the settings button (the gear) at the top right.
2. Under **Other calendars**, tap **Add a calendar**, then **Import a file (.ics)**, and pick the file.
3. On **Import calendar file**, untick what you don't want. Tap a date or time to change it.
4. Tap the add button at the bottom.

Or start from the file in Mail, Files or your downloads: share it to daili, or on Android open it with daili.

Past events and finished to-dos are left out. Entries you already have start unticked. Some repeat rules can't be copied, so those events are added once. To-dos go into the list you pick under **Add to**.

> Note: The file is read on this phone and not uploaded. Importing works in the phone app, not on the web.
