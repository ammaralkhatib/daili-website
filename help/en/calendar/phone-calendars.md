1. Open **Calendar**, tap the gear, then **Add a calendar** under **Other calendars**.
2. **Apple Calendar** (iPhone) or **Phone calendars** (Android): your phone's calendars.
3. **Google Calendar**: tap **Connect**, sign in, then pick calendars. **Only me** keeps one private.
4. **Subscribe with a link**: a school or work calendar, for everyone (admins) or **Only me**.
5. **Import a file (.ics)**: copies the events in once.

![Add calendar (link) with name, address and colour. The Only me switch is circled.](calendar-link-only-me)

A linked calendar's **Belongs to** sets its colour and its person in **Filter**. To fetch Google now, pull down, or **⋮** → **Refresh now**.

![A synced School trip from the linked School calendar. Edit in Daili is circled.](calendar-linked-event)

Tap an event → **Edit in Daili** to add people, reminders or notes. It stays linked (**From School**): new times, titles and places arrive by themselves. You changed it too? **Use it** or **Keep mine**. Deleted there? **Delete** or **Keep**. Linked events can't repeat.

> Note: Phone calendars stay on this phone. **Edit in Daili** hides the phone event here until you delete yours. No updates.
