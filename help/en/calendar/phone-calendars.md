1. Open **Calendar**, tap the gear, then **Add a calendar** under **Other calendars**.
2. **Apple Calendar** (iPhone) or **Phone calendars** (Android): your phone's own calendars.
3. **Google Calendar**: sign in, tap **Link**, then choose who sees each calendar.
4. **Subscribe with a link**: a school or club calendar that updates itself. Admins only. daili warns if your family has it.
5. **Import a file (.ics)**: copies the events into daili once.

![The Edit calendar sheet for a linked calendar. The Belongs to row, set to Emma, is circled.](calendar-other-calendars)

Each calendar has **Belongs to**: subscribed links start as **Everyone**, phone and Google calendars as you. Its events get that person's colour and follow **Filter**.

To change it, tap the phone or Google row, or a link's **⋮** → **Edit**. **⋮** → **Remove** (Google: **Disconnect**) takes one off.

These events can't be changed here. Tap one → **Copy to Daili** to make your own.

> Note: Phone calendars, and whom they belong to, stay on this phone: nothing is uploaded or shown to your family.
