Calendars you already have on this phone can show up next to your family's events.

1. Open **Calendar**.
2. Tap the settings button (the gear) at the top right.
3. Under **Phone calendars**, tap **Connect** and allow access.
4. Make sure **Show phone calendars** is on, then tap **Choose calendars** and pick the ones to show.

![The Phone calendars section in the calendar settings](calendar-phone-calendars)

> Note: These calendars stay on this phone. Nothing is uploaded, and the rest of your family doesn't see them.
