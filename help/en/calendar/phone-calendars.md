Work, school or sports calendars can show up next to your family's events.

1. Open **Calendar** and tap the gear at the top right.
2. Under **Other calendars**, tap **Add a calendar**.
3. **Apple Calendar** (iPhone) or **Phone calendars** (Android): your phone's own calendars. Allow access, and daili shows your main ones.
4. **Google Calendar**: sign in once and choose who sees each calendar.
5. **Subscribe with a link**: a school or club calendar that updates itself. Your whole family sees it. Admins only.
6. **Import a file (.ics)**: copies the events into daili once.

![The Add a calendar sheet with Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Subscribe with a link and Import a file (.ics).](calendar-other-calendars)

They're listed under **Other calendars**. The phone says **Only you**, links say **Family**. Tap the phone row to change your picks. To take it off, tap **⋮** → **Remove**. daili asks first. Nothing on your phone is deleted, and adding it again brings your picks back.

> Note: Phone calendars stay on this phone: nothing is uploaded, and your family doesn't see them.
