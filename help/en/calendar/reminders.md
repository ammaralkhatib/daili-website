A reminder is a notification before the event starts. An event can have up to 5.

![The event form. The Reminder row, with two reminders, is circled.](calendar-reminders)

1. In a new event, tap the **Reminder** row.
2. Tick one or more times, like 15 minutes or 1 day before. For another time, tap **Custom…**.
3. Tap **Done**, then **Save** on the event.

An all-day event offers **Morning of (9:00)**, **1 day before (9:00)** and **1 week before (9:00)**.

The form sets reminders for everyone. To change only yours, open the event and tap its reminder (also on app.daili.app). Tick your times, or none, in **Your reminder** and tap **Save**.

Want the same reminders on every new event? In **Calendar**, tap the gear at the top right. Under **Reminders**, tap **Default reminder** and tick up to 5. Keep **Event reminders** switched on there, or no reminders arrive on this phone.

> Note: A shared event reminds the people it's for. A private event only reminds you.
