A stock list holds what you always keep at home.

![A Stock list that sends missing items to Groceries, with Coffee and Milk running low or out. The Coffee row is circled.](lists-stock)

1. On Home, tap **Shopping**, then **+**, then **Stock list**. In **New stock list**, under **Missing items go to**, pick a shopping list, then tap **Create**.
2. Add what you keep at home.
3. When something runs low, tap it. It moves to **Running low or out** and goes onto your shopping list.
4. After you buy it, tick it on the shopping list. It's **In stock** again.

You can also start from a shopping list: tap an item and turn on **Keep in stock**.

Everyone who can see a stock list sees its items and what it adds to the shopping list. **Only me** keeps a stock list to yourself.

A stock list always has the box icon. Tapping and adding items here needs a connection.

> Note: People on an older version of daili don't see stock lists. They need to update the app.
