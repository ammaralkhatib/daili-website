Shampoo on the grocery list? Move it to the list where it belongs.

1. Open the list and tap the item.
2. Tap **Move to list…**.
3. Pick the other list. The item moves there, and a message says where it went.
4. Wrong list? Tap **Undo** in that message.

> Note: **Move to list…** only shows when your family has another list of the same kind. Shopping items move to shopping lists, and to-dos to to-do lists.
