New items get a category from their name, so milk lands in **Dairy**. Group the list by category and you walk through the shop once.

![The ⋯ menu of the Groceries list. The Group by category switch and the Categories row are circled.](lists-categories)

1. Open a shopping list and tap **⋯** at the top.
2. New shopping lists are already grouped. For an older list, turn on **Group by category**.
3. Tap **Categories**. Ticked ones are **In this list**, the rest **Not in this list**. **Turn all off** unticks all.
4. Drag them into your shop's order. Tap one to rename it, change its emoji or hide it. **Add category** makes your own, up to 30.
5. Tap an item to pick its **Category**. The icon next to it opens the same screen.

![The Categories screen of Groceries. Produce, Dairy, Bakery and Beverages are In this list, and the other 10 are Not in this list. The In this list card is circled.](lists-categories-per-list)

![The Edit item sheet for Milk. Only the list's 4 categories are offered. The categories button next to Category is circled.](lists-categories-item-sheet)

Each list keeps its own choice. An item keeps its category even when it's off. A category made inside a list is on there.

> Note: Only categories you added can be deleted: tap one, then **Delete category**. Its items stay.
