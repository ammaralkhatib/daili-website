New items get a category from their name, so milk lands in **Dairy**. Group the list by category and you walk through the shop once.

![The ⋯ menu of the Groceries list. The Group by category switch and the Categories row are circled.](lists-categories)

1. Open a shopping list and tap **⋯** at the top.
2. Turn on **Group by category**.
3. Tap **Categories** and drag them into the order you walk through your shop.
4. Tap a category to rename it, change its emoji or hide it. Tap **Add category** to make your own, up to 30.
5. To move one item, tap it, pick a **Category** and tap **Save**.

> Note: You can delete only the categories you added: tap one, then tap **Delete category**. Its items stay on your lists, just without a category.
