Done with a list but want to keep it? Archive it instead of deleting it. This works for shopping and to-do lists.

![The Archived lists screen with two lists. Restore in a list's menu is circled.](lists-archive)

1. On a list's card, tap **⋯** → **Archive**. Inside a list, it's in **⋯** too. **Undo** brings it right back.
2. Archived lists wait under **Archived · N** at the end of your lists. Tap one to read it. It can't be changed.
3. To bring one back, tap **⋯** next to it → **Restore**. **Delete** removes it for good.
4. To reuse a list, open it and tap **⋯** → **Save as template**.
5. Next time, tap **+** on your lists, then **Start from a template**.
6. To use, rename or delete templates: **⋯** at the top of your lists → **Templates**.

![The New list screen with Choose a template open, showing two templates. The template list is circled.](lists-templates)

A template keeps the items, without ticks, dates or people. Your family shares templates. One made from an **Only me** list stays yours.

> Note: An archived list sends no reminders.
