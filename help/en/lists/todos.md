Like a shopping list, but a task can also have a person, a due date and a reminder.

![The To-dos screen with one list. The Household list card is circled.](lists-todos)

1. On Home, tap **To-dos**, then tap a list. For a new list, tap **+**, type a **Name** and tap **Create**.
2. Type the task in **Add item** at the bottom and add it.
3. Tap a task to give it an **Assignee**, a **Due date**, a **Category** or a **Note**.
4. Done? Tap the box in front of the task. It moves down to **Completed**.
5. To sort the list, tap **⋯**, then **Group by**.

Swipe a task left to delete it, or press and hold to move it.

In a shared list, the lock next to the send arrow makes your next tasks private until you leave.

No signal? You can still tick and add tasks. Ticking a repeating task and other changes need a connection.

> Note: **Group by** is saved on this phone only.
