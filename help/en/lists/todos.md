Like a shopping list, but a task can also have a person, a due date and a reminder.

![The To-dos screen with one list. The Household list card is circled.](lists-todos)

1. On Home, tap **To-dos**, then tap a list. For a new list, tap **+**, type a **Name** and tap **Create**. **Only me** keeps it to yourself.
2. Type the task in **Add item** at the bottom and add it.
3. Tap a task to give it an **Assignee**, a **Due date**, a **Category** or a **Note**. Then tap **Save**.
4. Done? Tap the box in front of the task. It moves down to **Completed**.
5. To sort the list, tap **⋯** at the top, then **Group by**. Pick **Due date**, **Person** or **Category**.

In a shared list, the lock at the right of the add bar, just before the send arrow, makes your next tasks private until you leave the list. Open means off, closed means on.

> Note: **Group by** is saved on this phone only.
