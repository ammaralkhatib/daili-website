Everyone in your family sees the same shopping lists and every change.

![The Groceries list with open items and two ticked ones under Completed. The Add item bar at the bottom is circled.](lists-shopping)

1. On Home, tap **Shopping**, then tap a list. For a new list, tap **+**, then **Shopping list**, type a **Name** and tap **Create**.
2. Type what you need in **Add item** at the bottom and add it.
3. In the shop, tap the box in front of an item. It moves down to **Completed**.
4. Back home, tap **⋯**, then **Delete completed**. Or tap **Uncheck all** to use the same list again.

Swipe an item left to delete it, or press and hold to move it.

If you have a budget, ticking the last item offers to add the cost to it.

Track an item at home too: tap it, then **Keep in stock**.

No signal? Saved lists still open, and you can tick and add. A small cloud marks a row until it's sent. Other changes need a connection.
