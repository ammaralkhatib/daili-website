A task with a name on it gets done. You can give it to one person or to several.

![The Edit item sheet for a to-do. The Assignee field, set to Lena, is circled.](lists-assign)

1. Open a to-do list and tap the task.
2. Under **Assignee**, tap the field.
3. In **Assign to**, pick one or more people. Children without a login are there too.
4. Tap **Save**, then **Save** again on the task.

The task now shows their pictures, and it counts for each of them.

> Note: A reminder only goes to people with a login. A task given only to a child reminds everyone.
