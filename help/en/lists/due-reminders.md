A to-do can have a due day and a reminder. The reminder goes to the people the task is given to, or to everyone if nobody has it.

![A to-do's edit sheet with the reminder set to At due time. The Reminder field is circled.](lists-due-reminders)

1. While you type a new task, tap **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **Pick a date…** above the add bar.
2. For a reminder, tap the task. Under **Due date**, tap **Add due date** if it has none, and **Add time** for a time.
3. Under **Reminder**, pick when, like **At due time**. Then tap **Save**.
4. When the reminder comes, tap **Done** or **Snooze 15 min**. Tap the reminder to open the list at that to-do.

**Remind again every day until done** repeats it daily at the same time until the task is ticked off, up to 30 days past the due date. Overdue tasks too.

![A to-do's edit sheet. Remind again every day until done is circled.](lists-remind-daily)

> Note: **Reminder** only shows once the task has a due date. If you clear the due date, the reminder goes too.
