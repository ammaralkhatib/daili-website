A repeating to-do is one task that comes back. When you tick it, it moves on to its next due date.

![The Edit item sheet for a to-do with all the Repeat choices. Every few weeks is picked, every 2 weeks on Wednesday. The Repeat choices are circled.](lists-repeating)

1. Open a to-do list and tap the task.
2. Give it a **Due date**.
3. Under **Repeat**, pick **Daily**, **Every few days**, **Weekly**, **Every few weeks**, **Monthly**, **Every few months** or **Yearly**.
4. For **Weekly**, pick the days under **On these days**. For **Every few weeks**, also pick how many weeks.
5. For **Monthly**, pick the **Day of the month**. For **Every few months**, also pick how many months: 6 for every half year.
6. Tap **Save**.

**Yearly** comes back on the due date's day each year. When you tick a task off, a message tells you when it comes back.

> Note: **Repeat** is greyed out until the task has a due date. A repeating task needs one to move forward from.
