Everyone in your family sees the same calendar, lists and birthdays.

1. On Home, tap **Family**.
2. Tap **Invite member**.
3. Share the 6-digit code from **Code**, show the **QR code**, or tap **Share link**.
4. They open daili, tap **Join a family or group** and type the code, or tap **Scan QR code**.

![The Family screen with the Invite member button](family-invite)

> Note: A code works for 7 days, and the screen shows until when. After that, tap **Invite member** again for a new one.
