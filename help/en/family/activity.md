Anyone can change a shared event or list. The activity list shows who did it.

![The Activity list with rows like List archived and List restored. The Clear button is circled.](family-activity)

1. On Home, tap the bell at the top right. A number on it means there's something new.
2. Scroll the list. Changes are grouped under **Today**, **Yesterday** and older dates.
3. Tap a change to open the event, list or recipe it's about.
4. To empty the list, tap **Clear** at the top and confirm.

**Clear** clears the list only for you. Your family still sees everything. Entries are removed after 90 days anyway.

New changes have a light background the first time you see them. When someone shares a note with the whole family, you see **Note shared** here, without a notification. An archived or restored list says so.
