Anyone in the family can edit or delete shared events and list items. Roles only decide who runs the family.

![The Members list on the Family screen with Owner and Admin labels. The Admin label and the ⋮ button next to Marco are circled.](family-roles)

The **Owner** created the family. Only the owner can change roles, hand the family to someone else or delete it.

An **Admin** can remove members, manage children and change the family's settings. Parents are usually admins.

A **Member** uses everything, but doesn't manage the family. This fits kids and teens with their own phone.

1. On Home, tap **Family**.
2. Tap **⋮** next to a person and pick **Change role…** or **Remove from family**.
3. To hand the family over, tap **Transfer ownership** under **Ownership**.
4. To delete it for everyone, tap **Delete this family or group** there and type its name (and your password, if set). Others see a message on their next visit. Also on app.daili.app.

> Note: The new owner has to be an admin first. You become an admin after the transfer.
