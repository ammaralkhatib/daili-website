Anyone in the family can edit or delete shared events and list items.

![The Members list on the Family screen with Owner and Admin labels. The Admin label and the ⋮ button next to Marco are circled.](family-roles)

The **Owner** created the family. Only the owner can change roles, hand the family over or delete it.

An **Admin** (usually a parent) manages children, chores, reward asks, members and the family's settings.

A **Member**, like a teen with a phone, uses everything but doesn't manage the family.

When someone joins, Home asks the owner (until answered) if they are a parent: **Yes, make admin** or **No, keep as member**.

![Home with the sheet Marco joined your family, Is Marco a parent?, and the buttons No, keep as member and Yes, make admin.](family-ask-parent)

1. On Home, tap **Family**.
2. Tap **⋮** next to a person and pick **Change role…** or **Remove from family**.
3. To hand the family over, tap **Transfer ownership** under **Ownership**.
4. To delete it for everyone, tap **Delete this family or group** there and type its name (and your password, if set).

> Note: The new owner has to be an admin first. You become an admin after the transfer.
