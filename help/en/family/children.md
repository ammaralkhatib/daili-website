Young kids don't need their own account. You add them, and they show up under **Children**.

1. On Home, tap **Family**.
2. Under **Children**, tap **Add child**.
3. Type their **Name**, pick a **Color**, and tap **Add**.

Now you can pick them when you give someone a to-do.

When your child gets their own phone, an admin taps **⋮** next to their name, then **Convert to account…**, and types the child's email. They get an invite and keep their place in the family.

> Note: Only admins can change or delete a child. Tap **⋮** next to the child's name for **Edit** and **Delete**.
