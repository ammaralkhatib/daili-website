One account can be in up to 5 families or groups: your home, the grandparents, the sports club or the school class. Each has its own calendar and lists.

![The Your families & groups sheet over Home. The Create a family or group button is circled.](family-groups)

1. On Home, tap your family's name under the greeting.
2. Under **Your families & groups**, tap the one you want to open.
3. To add another one, tap **Create a family or group** or **Join with a code**.

You find the same list on the Family screen: tap **Switch family or group**.

> Note: Switching only changes this phone. Tap **⋮** next to a family and **Make default** to choose the one daili opens on a new phone.
