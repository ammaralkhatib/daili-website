Out of dinner ideas?

![The Plan tab. Plan my week is the first button under the week.](meals-plan-week)

1. On Home, tap **Meal plan**, then **Plan my week**.
2. Pick filters: **Quick (under 30 min)**, **Kid-friendly**, **Vegetarian**, **Only my recipes**. Add a wish under **Anything to use up or avoid?**
3. Tap **Suggest dinners**.
4. On **Your week, suggested**, untick days you skip. **Swap** gives another idea. **Try again** reopens the filters for new ideas (one more suggestion).
5. Tap the add button, like **Add 5 dinners**. **Add to shopping list** takes the week's ingredients to a list.

Only open evenings from today are filled, skipping the last 7 days' meals. New ideas join your Recipes.

> Note: AI is for adults (18+). Your recipe titles, recent meals and wish go to Google's AI, which stores nothing. daili asks you first, and **Settings** → **AI features (Google Gemini)** switches it off.

> Note: A suggestion uses one AI action. Every family gets 3 AI actions a month free; Daili Plus gives 30.
