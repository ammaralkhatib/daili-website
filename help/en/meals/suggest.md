![The Plan tab. AI plan in the bar at the bottom is circled.](meals-suggest)

1. On Home, tap **Meal plan**, then **AI plan**.
2. Pick filters: **Quick (under 30 min)**, **Kid-friendly**, **Vegetarian**, **Only my recipes**. Type anything to use up or avoid.
3. Tap **Suggest dinners**.
4. On **Your week, suggested**, untick days you skip. **Swap** gives another idea. **Try again** reopens the filters for new ideas (one more suggestion).
5. Tap the add button, like **Add 3 dinners**. **Add to shopping list** adds the ingredients to a list.

Only open evenings from today are filled, skipping the last 7 days' meals.

With family wishes, a switch puts them in first, one per day, if they fit dinner.

> Note: AI is for adults (18+). Your recipe titles, recent meals, wishes and what you type go to Google's AI, which stores nothing. daili asks you first, and **Settings** → **AI features (Google Gemini)** switches it off.

> Note: A suggestion uses one AI action: 3 a month are free, Daili Plus gives 30.
