Pancakes again? Let everyone say what they'd like to eat.

![The Plan tab with the Wishes sheet open, showing two wishes with Plan buttons. The list of wishes is circled.](meals-wishes)

1. On Home, tap **Meal plan**, then **Wishes** at the bottom.
2. Tap **Add a wish**. Pick a recipe or type a meal, then tap **Add wish**.
3. To plan one, tap **Plan** next to it, pick a day and a meal, and confirm.

A day's add sheet also lists wishes, under **Family wishes**. The planned meal shows whose wish it was. If someone else plans it, the wisher is notified.

**AI plan** can use wishes. They go in first, one per day, if they fit dinner.

Only you and admins can edit or delete your wishes. Admins can also add a wish for a child without a login.

On app.daili.app, wishes sit in a row above the week, with **+** to add one. Click a wish, then **Plan it**.

> Note: Don't want the notification? Switch off **Your meal wishes** in **Settings** → **Notifications**.
