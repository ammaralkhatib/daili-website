Not every evening needs cooking. Plan that too, so everyone knows.

![The add sheet on an empty dinner with Leftovers, Eating out and Takeaway, and the Recent | All recipes switch. The Leftovers chip is circled.](meals-add-meal)

1. On **Meal plan**, tap **+** on a day.
2. Tap **Leftovers** and pick the meal they're from, out of the 3 days before. Or tap **Eating out** or **Takeaway**.

Plans changed for a meal that's already planned? Tap it. **Eat out instead** moves the dish to the next free dinner. **Leftovers instead** first asks which meal they're from, then does the same. **Cook double** doubles the servings and puts the leftovers on the next free dinner after it. One **Undo** puts everything back.

The meals widget then shows "Eating out" or "Leftovers: Lasagne" for tonight.

On app.daili.app, quick add and the meal panel have the same buttons.
