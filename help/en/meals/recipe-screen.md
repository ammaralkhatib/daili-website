A recipe shows more than its steps: what the family thinks of it, and how often it was on the menu.

![Grandma's lasagne with Who likes it. Two hearts and one sad face are shown, and faces without an answer are dimmed. It also says On the menu 6 times. The Who likes it row is circled.](meals-recipe-screen)

1. On Home, tap **Recipes**, then a recipe.
2. Under **Who likes it**, tap your own face, then **Love it** or **Not for me**.
3. In **Ingredients**, tap **+** or **−** to change the servings. The amounts change with them.
4. Under the steps, tap **Add a note for your family** to write tips or changes.

A face stays dimmed until that person answers. Admins answer for children without a login.

![Vegetable curry set to 6 servings. It says Scaled from 4 and the amounts have changed (1½ small pumpkin). The Ingredients card is circled.](meals-recipe-servings)

Other servings aren't saved: it opens with its own number, or the planned meal's. **Add all to list** and the cart button at the top take the new amounts. A line daili can't change is marked **not scaled**.

![The end of a recipe, under the steps. The Add a note for your family field is circled.](meals-recipe-notes)

**Our notes** belong to the whole family, and anyone can change them: tap the pencil, or find them when you edit the recipe.
