Found a good recipe online? Save it without typing it out.

![The Recipes screen with the + menu open. Import from link is circled.](meals-import-link)

1. Copy the link to the recipe page.
2. On Home, tap **Recipes**, then **+**, then **Import from link**.
3. Paste the link under **Recipe link**, or tap **Paste**. Then tap **Import**.
4. Check what daili found and fix anything that's off. Then tap **Save**.

If a page has no recipe data, daili still saves the title and photo. Add the ingredients and steps yourself.

Faster: in your browser or the YouTube app, share the page to **daili** and pick **Recipe**.

> Note: TikTok and Facebook links don't contain recipe data. Copy the recipe text instead, and use **Paste recipe text**. For YouTube or Instagram, use **From YouTube or Instagram**.
