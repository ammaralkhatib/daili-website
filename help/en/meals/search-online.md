Out of ideas? Search recipe sites for any dish without leaving daili.

![Search online before typing, with idea rows like Curry night and Quick dinners and a cooking time on every card. The search field is circled.](meals-search-online)

1. On Home, tap **Recipes**, then **+**, then **Search online**.
2. Before you type, browse the idea rows, like **Quick dinners**. **See more** shows more.
3. Or type what you'd like to cook, like lasagne, and tap **Search**. To narrow it down, tap **Vegan** or **Vegetarian**.
4. Tap a result. daili imports it for you. Or tap **Save and plan** on a result to save it and plan it at once.
5. Check the recipe and tap **Save**.

Most cards show the cooking time. Recipes come from blogs in your language. A small language tag marks the rare one that isn't. Round-ups and weekly menus are filtered out.

> Note: Online search needs a verified email address. If yours isn't verified yet, the screen says so and has a **Resend** button for the link. There is also a daily limit on searches.
