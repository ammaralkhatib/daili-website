On **Recipes**, tap the magnifier and type a word. Search looks in the title and in every ingredient, so "pumpkin" finds every recipe that uses pumpkin.

![A search for pumpkin. The vegetable curry shows Contains: 1 small pumpkin.](meals-search-ingredient)

A recipe found by an ingredient shows **Contains: …**. The meal plan's recipe picker searches the same way.

Newest recipes come first. The arrows at the top switch to **Name A–Z** or **Quickest first**, and your phone remembers.

Tags are your family's own labels, like Party or Gluten-free. The whole family shares up to 50.

![The filter row with Party picked. Only the lasagne and the pancakes show.](meals-tags)

1. Tap **New tag** in the filter row, or under **Tags** in the recipe form. Name it, pick a colour and tap **Save**.
2. Open a recipe, tap **⋮** → **Edit**, tap its tags, then **Save**.
3. Tap tags or categories in the filter row. With several picked, you see recipes with any of them. **Clear** shows all again.

To rename, recolour or delete a tag, long-press it or tap **Manage tags**.
