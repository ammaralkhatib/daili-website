Flour on your fingers? Cook mode stops the screen from going dark.

![A recipe with the Cook mode switch above the ingredients. The switch is circled.](meals-cook-mode)

1. Open a recipe.
2. Switch on **Cook mode**, above the ingredients.

The screen now stays on while the recipe is open.

Cook mode is off each time you open a recipe. It turns itself off after 1 hour without a touch.
