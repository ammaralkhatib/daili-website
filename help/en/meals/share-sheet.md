Found something in another app? Send it to daili.

![Save to daili for a shared message. The Note | Recipe | Event switch is circled.](meals-share-sheet)

1. In the other app, tap Share, then **daili**.
2. In **Save to daili**, pick **Note**, **Recipe** or **Event**.
3. Tap **Save** for a note, or **Continue** for a recipe or an event.

**Recipe**: daili reads a link from a recipe site, your browser or YouTube. It sorts text into ingredients and steps. A photo is read by AI (1 AI action), only the first picture for now.

**Event**: daili's AI reads a message, say from WhatsApp or Signal, or a screenshot, and suggests the event (1 AI action). Found several? Untick the ones you don't need.

You check everything first. Nothing is saved until you confirm it. Recipe links start on **Recipe**; for pictures, daili starts on your last choice.

> Note: AI is for adults (18+); daili asks once.
