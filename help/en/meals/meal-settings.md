Tell daili how your family eats.

![Meal settings with Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snack switches, usual servings and dinner time. The meal switches are circled.](meals-meal-settings)

1. On Home, tap **Meal plan**, then the tune icon at the top (**Meal settings**).
2. Under **Meals you plan**, switch on **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner** or **Snack**. At least one stays on.
3. **Usual servings**: your family size. Recipes scale to it.
4. **Dinner time**: when you usually eat.

These switches pick your main meal (dinner, if it's on), which a day's **+** starts on. **Plan** on a recipe and **Move or swap** still offer every meal, and the week shows all you planned.

The dinner time also finds busy evenings. An event with a time, in any calendar daili shows, from 90 minutes before dinner until 30 minutes after marks that day, and quick meals come first.

These settings are for the whole family, and everyone in it can change them. On app.daili.app, they're in the **⋯** menu of the meal plan.
