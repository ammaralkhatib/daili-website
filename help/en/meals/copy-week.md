Lots of families eat the same things most weeks. Copy them instead of adding each meal again.

![The Plan tab. Copy last week is the last button in the row under the week.](meals-plan-week)

1. On Home, tap **Meal plan** and go to the week you want to fill.
2. Swipe the row under the week and tap **Copy last week**.
3. If the week already has meals, tap **Copy** to confirm.

It copies the meals from the week before the one you're looking at.

> Note: Copying only adds meals. The meals already in the week stay.
