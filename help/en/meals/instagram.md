Found a recipe on Instagram? daili saves it for you, on iPhone and Android.

![The Recipes screen with the + menu open. From YouTube or Instagram is circled.](meals-instagram)

1. In Instagram, tap Share on the post, then **daili**, then **Continue**. On iPhone, daili is under **More** the first time.
2. Or copy the post's link. On Home, tap **Recipes**, then **+**, then **From YouTube or Instagram**. Paste the link and tap **Read**.
3. Check the recipe and the post's picture, then tap **Create**.

daili reads only the post's text, not its pictures or video. No written recipe, or **Couldn't read this post** (for example offline)? Tap **Choose screenshots** and pick screenshots of the recipe.

Your phone opens the post. Only its text goes to Google's AI, which stores nothing.

> Note: Only public posts. Reading uses one AI action: 3 a month are free, Daili Plus gives 30. AI is for adults (18+); daili asks once. Without AI, the text opens in **Paste recipe text**, sorted on your phone.
