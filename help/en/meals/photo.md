Have a recipe on paper or in a screenshot? daili can type it up for you.

![The Recipes screen with the + menu open. From a photo is circled.](meals-photo)

1. On Home, tap **Recipes**, then **+**, then **From a photo**.
2. Tap **Take photo** or **Choose from library**. From the library, pick up to 5 pictures of one recipe.
3. Check the library pictures, then tap **Read this photo** or **Read 3 pictures**.
4. The **New recipe** form opens, already filled in. Check the amounts, then tap **Create**.

The recipe comes out in daili's language, whatever language the page is in. Printed or clearly written text works best.

> Note: AI is for adults (18+); daili asks once. The pictures go to Google's AI to be read, then are deleted, without camera data like location. daili asks you first, and **Settings** → **AI features (Google Gemini)** switches it off.

> Note: A read uses one AI action, even with 5 pictures: 3 a month are free, Daili Plus gives 30.
