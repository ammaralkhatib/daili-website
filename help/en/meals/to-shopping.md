No more copying ingredients by hand.

![A recipe with its ingredients. The shopping cart button at the top is circled.](meals-to-shopping)

1. Open a recipe and tap the shopping cart button at the top. Or on **Meal plan**, tap **Shop** at the bottom to take the whole week.
2. Under **Add to**, pick the shopping list.
3. Untick anything you already have at home.
4. Tap the button at the bottom, like **Add 3 items**.

Tap **Open list** in the message to see them.

**Add all to list** under a recipe's ingredients does the same. If you changed the servings, the new amounts go on the list.

From **Meal plan**, the amounts follow each meal's **Servings**. A recipe for 4 planned for 8 says "for 8" and its amounts double. A line without an amount gets "2×".

![Add to shopping list for this week. Vegetable curry says Dinner · Wed, Nov 18 · for 8 and its amounts are doubled. The for 8 line is circled.](meals-week-shopping)

> Note: From the meal plan, only meals made from a recipe bring ingredients. Meals without a recipe have none.
