On a computer, you can use daili in your browser. Same account, same family: every change shows up on everyone's phones.

1. Open app.daili.app.
2. Sign in with your email and password, or tap **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**. **Forgot password?** and **Restore my account** work here too.
3. Use the menu on the left: calendar, shopping, to-dos, habits, recipes, meal plan, notes, budget, birthdays and your family.

Notes work here too: write, filter, tag, pin, share, set a reminder (it shows on the calendar) and restore recently deleted ones. Home's quick capture can make a note. Daili Plus families can add pictures.

To delete your account, click your name, then **Delete account** under **Account**.

Documents, Photos, recording voice and the notes' AI helpers stay in the phone app.

> Note: The browser version is made for computers and tablets. On a phone, it asks you to use the app instead.
