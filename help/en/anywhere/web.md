On a computer, you can use daili in your browser. It's the same account and the same family, so everything you change shows up on everyone's phones.

1. On your computer, open app.daili.app in your browser.
2. Sign in with your email and password, or tap **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**.
3. Use the menu on the left: calendar, shopping, to-dos, habits, recipes, meal plan, birthdays and your family.

Documents and Photos stay in the phone app, because they live only on your phone. Deleting your account or a family is done in the app too.

> Note: The browser version is made for computers. On a phone or tablet, it asks you to use the app instead.
