On a computer, you can use daili in your browser. It's the same account and the same family, so everything you change shows up on everyone's phones.

1. On your computer, open app.daili.app in your browser.
2. Sign in with your email and password, or tap **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**.
3. Use the menu on the left: calendar, shopping, to-dos, habits, recipes, meal plan, notes, birthdays and your family.

Notes work here too: write, filter, tag, pin, share, set a reminder (it shows on the calendar) and restore recently deleted ones. Home's quick capture can make a note. Daili Plus families can add pictures.

Documents, Photos, recording voice and the notes' AI helpers stay in the phone app. So does deleting your account or a family.

> Note: The browser version is made for computers. On a phone or tablet, it asks you to use the app instead.
