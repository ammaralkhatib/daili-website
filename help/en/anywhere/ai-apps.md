Ask your AI app "What's on this weekend?" or "Put milk on the shopping list".

**In ChatGPT:** open **Apps**, search for **Daili** and connect it.

**In Claude:**

1. Open **Customize** → **Connectors** → **Add custom connector**.
2. Name it Daili, paste https://api.daili.app/mcp and press **Add**.
3. Press **Connect**.

Other AI apps that support MCP use the same address.

Then sign in, pick the family or group and press **Allow**.

It reads your calendar, lists, meals, notes, birthdays and family, adds events, to-dos, shopping items, meals and notes, and ticks items off. It can't edit or delete.

> Note: It sees only what you see in that family or group, never others' private items, Budget, Cycle, Documents, Photos or Google and Outlook events. What it reads goes to the AI app's company.

To stop, tap **Disconnect** in **Settings** → **Connected AI apps** (web: **Profile & settings**).

If you need Daili Plus, the sign-in window says so.
