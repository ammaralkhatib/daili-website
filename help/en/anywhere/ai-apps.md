Ask Claude or another AI app "What's on this weekend?" or "Put milk on the shopping list".

1. In Claude, open **Customize** → **Connectors** → **Add custom connector**.
2. Name it Daili, paste https://api.daili.app/mcp and press **Add**.
3. Press **Connect**, sign in, pick the family or group, then press **Allow**.

Other AI apps that support MCP use the same address.

It reads your calendar, lists, meals, notes, birthdays and family, adds events, to-dos, shopping items, meals and notes, and ticks items off. It can't edit or delete.

> Note: It sees only what you see in that family or group. Others' private items, Budget, Cycle, Documents and Photos, and Google or Outlook events never reach it. What it reads goes to the AI app's company.

To stop, tap **Disconnect** in **Settings** → **Connected AI apps** (web: **Profile & settings**), or leave the family or group.

If you need Daili Plus, the sign-in window says so.
