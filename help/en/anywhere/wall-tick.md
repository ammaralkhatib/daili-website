On a connected family screen, tap a to-do, a shopping item or a habit to tick it.

It counts for the person it belongs to. A shopping item, a to-do for nobody or for several people, and a habit you do together ask **Who did it?** Tap your face.

Children without a login get the credit for their own habits. For their to-dos, the activity on the website shows their name.

**Undo** shows for 5 seconds. A ticked row is not a button, so use **Undo** to take a tick back. Later, untick it on a computer or in the app. A repeating to-do moves to its next date at once and has no **Undo**, the same as in the app.

> Note: A board opened with **Start wall mode** under your own sign-in only shows things. Ticking works on a family screen.
