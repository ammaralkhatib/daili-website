On a connected family screen, tap a to-do, a shopping item or a habit to tick it.

It counts for the person it belongs to. A shopping item, a to-do for nobody or for several people, and a habit you do together ask **Who did it?** Tap your face. Children without a login get the credit for their own habits.

**Undo** shows for 5 seconds. It's the only way to take a tick back here. A repeating to-do has no **Undo**.

The round **+** at the top right adds an event, to-do, shopping item or meal. Pick who's adding first.

Rewards sit under each person's habits. A parent taps **We did it** on a full one. **Reward** under a column adds a new one. Both ask **Who is this?** first.

> Note: A board opened with **Open the board here** or **Start wall mode** under your own sign-in only shows things.
