An old tablet becomes a family screen: **Home**, **Calendar** (Day · Week · Month), **Meals**, **To-dos**, **Shopping** and **Habits**, with the weather on top. It dims to **Night** at its parent's quiet hours.

1. On the tablet, open app.daili.app/board/pair. A code and a QR appear.
2. In the app (admins): **Settings** → **Wall display**. **Copy** copies that address.
3. Tap **Connect a screen**. Type the code and tap **Continue**, or **Scan QR code**. Then **Approve**.

![Wall display with the tablet address app.daili.app/board/pair and Copy, then Connect a screen, Wall screens and Setup guide. The address card is circled.](anywhere-wall)

**Setup guide** opens daili.app/wall-display: tips for each tablet. On a computer: **Wall display** in the left menu.

Tap an event for its details, or a face for one person's plans. A recipe's **Cook** fills the screen.

> Note: It never shows private things, not even yours, nor Notes, Budget, Cycle or Documents.

To stop: **Wall display** → **Wall screens** → **Remove**. Or on the tablet, tap the exit icon at the bottom left, hold **Hold to leave**, then **Stop using**.
