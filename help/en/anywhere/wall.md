An old tablet becomes a family screen: **Home**, **Week**, **Month**, **People**, **Meals**, **Lists**, **Habits** and **Countdown**. It dims to **Night** at its parent's quiet hours.

1. On the tablet, open app.daili.app/board/pair. A code and a QR appear.
2. In the app: **Settings** → **Wall display** → **Connect a screen** (admins only). Type the code, or **Scan QR code**.
3. Tap **Approve**.

A computer as the screen: your name → **Preferences** → **Use this browser as a family screen**.

The first tap goes full screen. The board restarts and reconnects by itself.

> Note: It never shows private things, not even yours, nor Notes, Budget, Cycle or Documents.

Remove it: **Wall display** → **Wall screens** → **Remove**. It also stops if its parent leaves or loses admin. On the tablet: **Hold to leave**, then **Stop using**.

iPad: Add to Home Screen, open it, tap **Set up a family screen**. Auto-Lock: Never. Android: Chrome → Install app.
