An old tablet becomes a big-letter family screen: **Home**, **Week**, **Month**, **People**, **Meals**, **Lists**, **Habits** and **Countdown**. At its parent's quiet hours, it dims to **Night**.

1. On the tablet, open app.daili.app/board/pair. A code and a QR appear.
2. On your phone or computer, scan the QR or type the code at that address.
3. Tap **Approve**. Only a parent (admin) can. The tablet needs no password.

To make a computer the screen: your name → **Preferences** → **Use this browser as a family screen**.

> Note: It never shows private things, not even yours, nor Notes, Budget, Cycle or Documents.

To remove it: **Family** → **Wall screens** → **Remove**. It also stops if its parent leaves the family or loses admin. On the tablet, hold **Hold to leave** for two seconds, then **Stop using**.

iPad: Add to Home Screen, open it, tap **Set up a family screen**. Auto-Lock: Never. Android: Chrome → Install app.
