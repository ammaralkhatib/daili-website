The **Shopping**, **To-dos** and **Habits** widgets have settings. Each widget keeps its own, so two Shopping widgets can show two lists.

1. Press and hold the widget.
2. On iPhone, tap **Edit Widget**. On Android, tap the pencil.
3. Pick what it shows. On Android, tap **Done**.

**Shopping**: under **List**, pick **Most recent** or one of your lists.

**To-dos**: under **Show**, pick **Mine** or **Everyone**.

**Habits**: under **Show**, pick **Family** or **Only mine**. Minzi and the energy bar always show the whole family.

A widget always follows the family or group that's active in daili — there is no family choice per widget.

> Note: On iPhone, settings and ticking on the widget need iOS 17 or later.
