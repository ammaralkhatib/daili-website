A menu on the left holds your places. An open list, recipe or day shows beside its overview. Most forms open as a window.

Turn the tablet either way, or split the screen at any size: what you opened and typed stays. Narrow windows and phones, even sideways, keep the phone layout.

Shortcuts, with Cmd (iPad) or Ctrl (Android):

1. **N** or **Cmd/Ctrl+N**: add something new, on most screens.
2. **/** or **Cmd/Ctrl+F**: search Recipes, Notes, Birthdays or Help.
3. **T**: today in the calendar. Arrows: a day or week in Month, a page in other views.
4. **Cmd/Ctrl+Enter**: press a form's green **Save** or **Create** button.
5. **Cmd/Ctrl+1** to **9**: menu places. **Cmd/Ctrl+,**: **Settings**.
6. **Esc**: close a window.

Single keys never fire while you type.

Mouse or trackpad: hover lights up, right-click works like a long-press, and you can drag Month events.

> Note: A wall screen is different: a family board on a tablet.
