TRMNL is an e-ink wall screen. It shows one screen at a time and refreshes itself. daili is a free plugin for it.

1. In TRMNL, open **Plugins**, find **Daili** and install it.
2. Enter your daili email (it must be confirmed), then the 6-digit code we send you. In several families or groups? Pick one.
3. Open the plugin's settings in TRMNL. Pick a **Screen**: Today, 3 days, Week, Month or List. Turn on **Show weather** if you like, then press **Save and update screen**.

For several screens, add Daili more than once; TRMNL rotates them. Daili also fits half or a quarter of the screen.

![The Today screen on a TRMNL display: events, to-dos, dinner and weather.](anywhere-trmnl)

> Note: Private events, lists and to-dos never appear. Budget, Cycle, Habits and Notes never show.

> Note: Turned the display upright (or back)? It fits itself at the next refresh, or press **Save and update screen**.

To stop, press **Disconnect** in the plugin's settings, then remove it in TRMNL.
