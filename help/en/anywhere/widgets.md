daili has three widgets: **Up next** shows the next entries from your family calendar, **Shopping** shows your latest shopping list, and **To-dos** shows what's due today, overdue and coming up.

1. Press and hold an empty spot on your phone's home screen.
2. On iPhone, tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**. On Android, tap **Widgets**.
3. Find daili in the list and tap it.
4. Pick a widget and a size. On iPhone, tap **Add Widget**. On Android, drag it to a free spot.

Tap a widget to open that part of daili.

> Note: A widget shows what daili last loaded. If one looks out of date, open daili for a moment.
